# GTA 6 Coffee Break Walkthrough

by [PowerPyx](https://www.powerpyx.com/author/powerpyx/) [Leave a Comment](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-coffee-break-walkthrough/#respond)

**Coffee Break** is the 40th main mission in GTA 6 (Grand Theft Auto VI). This walkthrough will guide you through all objectives of the **Coffee Break** main quest.

* **Starting Location:** Kelly County > Starlet Motel
* **Chapter:** 2
* **Quest Giver:** Jason/Lucia
* **Requirement:** Finished Chapter 2 “[Breakfast Run](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-breakfast-run-check-on-jason-walkthrough/)“.
* **Can Be Started By:** Jason & Lucia together
* **Reward:** can now freely switch between playing Lucia or Jason
* **Quest Description:** After their string of stick ups, Jason and Lucia decide to keep rolling together and hit something bigger so they can move to Vice City and stay with Jason’s high school friend, Dre’Quan.

**Objectives:**

1. Go to the motel kitchen with Lucia
2. Buy Lucia a coffee
3. Go to your car
4. Hold ![:right:](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-includes/images/smilies/right.png) to interact with your partner

## Starting Location: Coffee Break

The mission starts immediately after Chapter 2 “[Breakfast Run](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-breakfast-run-check-on-jason-walkthrough/)” (seamless mission transition). Jason & Lucia will spawn outside Starlet Motel, that’s when the mission officially starts.

[![](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-content/uploads/gta-6-walkthrough-coffee-break-2-300x169.jpg)](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-content/uploads/gta-6-walkthrough-coffee-break-2.jpg) [![](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-content/uploads/gta-6-walkthrough-coffee-break-3-300x169.jpg)](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-content/uploads/gta-6-walkthrough-coffee-break-3.jpg)

## Go to the motel kitchen with Lucia

The motel kitchen will be straight in front of where you spawn, in the southern corner of the motel property. On the way to the kitchen you will run into Wyman who recommends checking out the Mud Club for dirt races.

[![](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-content/uploads/gta-6-walkthrough-coffee-break-7-300x169.jpg)](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-content/uploads/gta-6-walkthrough-coffee-break-7.jpg) [![](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-content/uploads/gta-6-walkthrough-coffee-break-8-300x169.jpg)](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-content/uploads/gta-6-walkthrough-coffee-break-8.jpg)

## Buy Lucia a coffee

Lucia will sit down at the table. Interact with the coffee machine and buy the Coffee for $2. It will automatically start a cutscene where the couple goes over a plan what to do next.
