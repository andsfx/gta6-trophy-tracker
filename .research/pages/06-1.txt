# GTA 6 (Grand Theft Auto VI) Trophy Guide & Roadmap

by [PowerPyx](https://www.powerpyx.com/author/powerpyx/) [1 Comment](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-grand-theft-auto-vi-trophy-guide-roadmap/#comments)

## GTA 6 (Grand Theft Auto VI) Trophy Roadmap

* **Estimated trophy difficulty**: 4/10
* **Approximate amount of time to platinum**: 200 hours
* **Offline Trophies**: 55 (1, 2, 6, 46)
* **Online Trophies**: 0
* **Number of missable trophies**: Potentially 1 –  [**Positive Mental Attitude**](#positive-mental-attitude) as the first meditation is missable in Chapter 1 (see note in Step 1)
* **Glitched trophies**: 0
* **Does difficulty affect trophies?**: No, there are no difficulty settings, it’s one and the same difficulty for everyone
* **Hardest Trophy**:  A Long Road (attain [100% completion](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-100-completion-checklist-guide/))
* **Minimum Playthroughs**: 1
* **PS4/PS5 Autopop, Save Transfer, Crossbuy:** No, game doesn’t have a PS4 version
* **Free-Roam / Level Select after Story?:** There is still free-roam after the story, but no mission replay
* **Do Cheats disable trophies?:** Yes, Cheats disable trophies and saving the game – see [Cheats List](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-all-cheats/)
* **New Game+ Mode?:** No
* **Supports Manual Saves?:** Yes, 15 Manual Save Slots & 1 Autosave Slot – because there’s no mission replay it’s recommended to keep a save at the start and end of each chapter (10 saves), then rotate between the remaining 5 saves for your free-roam cleanup.
* **Release Date:** November 19, 2026
