# Positive Mental Attitude Trophy in Grand Theft Auto VI

- #### Positive Mental Attitude

 

Meditate at every spot across Leonida.

## How to unlock Positive Mental Attitude

**Potentially Missable**

Firstly, the reason we mark this as potentially missable is because the first Meditation Spot is really easy to just skip. The first instance comes when Lucia gets home from prison, when the pair are living in Key Lento, and one morning she asks you to meditate down on Jason’s dock. If you did, great! If you didn’t, we’re not sure whether it counts, but it definitely counts as a Meditation Spot in the in-game stats, of which there are 18 (well, there’s 8 in total, which you do as both Jason and Lucia; and the first and last as a duo, which count as one each).

Luckily you don’t need all 18 for the GTA VI’s 100% Completion (see A Long Road ), though. So, if it is missable, you can easily start a new save and make a beeline for these and get them in an hour or so, max. Anyway! There are 10 Meditation Spots, the location of which can be found through the link below. Once you’ve been to all 10 Meditation Spots and completed a meditation, the ‘Positive Mental Attitude’ trophy will unlock.
