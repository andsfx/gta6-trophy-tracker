# GTA 6 All Strangers & Freaks Side Missions

by [PowerPyx](https://www.powerpyx.com/author/powerpyx/) [Leave a Comment](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-all-strangers-freaks-side-missions/#respond)

Strangers & Freaks are the Side Missions in GTA 6 (Grand Theft Auto VI). Completing 8 mission strands for strangers across the state of Leonida unlocks the **Strangers & Freaks** trophy and achievement.

They are typically labeled as Optional Missions in-game on the map quest list or marked as purple side activities. Some are met during the story and the game marks their questline for you, others are completely hidden and unmarked. For the **Strangers & Freaks** only certain ones count and the only way to keep track is to check the trophy counter before and after their last quest (via the trophy list). The ones that do count are marked green below.

Some of the others have own trophy for finishing their questline (Kenton, Bret Vogel, Andy, Billy, Tyrone). After completing a side quest, it takes a while for their next quest to appear. Do other tasks until they reach out over the phone and their next quest will get marked. Sometimes this can take multiple weeks in-game time or even span across multiple chapters (for example, Kirk’s questline can be started in Chapter 1 but can’t be finished until Chapter 2).

The listed chapters are when their questline can first be started. Unless marked as missable they are still available after the story. You can also start their questline in one chapter and complete it later in another chapter, the quests remain marked permanently. All the trophy-related questlines are still there after the story in free-roam. The missable ones don’t count toward trophies and aren’t needed for 100% completion. All of these add toward the 100 optional tasks on the [100% Completion Checklist](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-100-completion-checklist-guide/), but there are a lot more than 100 as it counts every purple marker including each car delivery and Meditation.
