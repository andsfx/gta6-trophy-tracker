# GTA 6 Veteran Hunted (Meet Bret) Walkthrough

by [PowerPyx](https://www.powerpyx.com/author/powerpyx/) [Leave a Comment](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-veteran-hunted-meet-bret-walkthrough/#respond)

**Veteran Hunted (Meet Bret)** is a Strangers and Freaks side mission in GTA 6 (Grand Theft Auto VI). This walkthrough will guide you through all objectives of the **Veteran Hunted (Meet Bret)** side quest.

* **Starting Location:** Lummox County
* **Chapter:** 2
* **Quest Giver:** Bret Vogel
* **Requirement:** At some point after Chapter 2 “[Coffee Break](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-coffee-break-walkthrough/)“, while accumulating $15,000 for “[Prepare for Vice City](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-prepare-for-vice-city-walkthrough/)“, Jason will get a text message from Bret Vogel that marks this on the map. You have to play as Jason to get the next.
* **Can Be Started By:** Jason Only
* **Reward:** $500 from Bret via BuckMe + Unique Weapon Variant “Killtelligence SM-69”.
* **Quest Description:** Jason’s old friend, Bret Vogel, wants a hand with his hunting business near Mount Kalaga.
* **Questline (Bret):** [**Veteran Hunted**](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-veteran-hunted-meet-bret-walkthrough/) > Bret’s Target Decks

1. Get in the truck
2. Focus & Follow the animal tracks
3. Equip a sniper rifle or binoculars
4. Study the deer
5. Take out the deer using your hunting rifle
6. Pick up the deer carcass
7. Wait for Bret / Stow the deer in Bret’s truck
8. Get in the truck / drive back to the cabin
9. Unload the deer from Bret’s truck / Drop the deer in the processing area

## Starting Location: Veteran Hunted (Meet Bret)

At some point after Chapter 2 “[Coffee Break](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-coffee-break-walkthrough/)“, while accumulating $15,000 for “[Prepare for Vice City](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-prepare-for-vice-city-walkthrough/)“, Jason will get a text message from Bret Vogel that marks this on the map. You have to play as Jason, only he can receive the text message (not Lucia). The mission then gets marked on the map in the very north, in Lummox County. Jason has to go there alone, you can’t start the mission if Lucia is with you. Bret is an old friend of Jason, they served together in the army.
