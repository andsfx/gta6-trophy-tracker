## How to unlock Just Like Paradise

Story-related, cannot miss.

You’ll unlock the ‘Just Like Paradise’ trophy once you complete GTA 6’s first chapter, “Gone Fishing.” After taking Cal back home and returning to Jason’s home, this will unlock in the morning, while you’re on the phone to the gun store.
