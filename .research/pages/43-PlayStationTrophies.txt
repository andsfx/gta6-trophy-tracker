# Pest Control Trophy in Grand Theft Auto VI

- #### Pest Control

 

Rob an Illegal Operation belonging to each gang.

## How to unlock Pest Control

There are 9 gangs in GTA VI: the Kingfishers, Kolt & Associates, M7, La Polilla Negra, Final Chapter, SAN4SAN, PTT Youngin$, The Guardia Brothers and “Criminals” – basically, independents. You have to hit an Illegal Operation of each of theirs to unlock this trophy.

Illegal Operations are the yellow icons and zones on the map, and usually have lots of gang members inside, but also a ton of contraband, Unique Weapon Variants, Unique Weapons, Luxury Watches, and money. Lots of money.

Considering you need to hit all 39 Illegal Operations to achieve GTA VI’s 100% completion (see A Long Road ), this will easily come along the way. The location of all Illegal Operations is as follows:

- GTA VI – All Illegal Operations Locations (Plus, All Key Valuables)
