TITLE: GTA 6 UFO Aliens Easter Egg Location

In GTA 6 (Grand Theft Auto VI) you can find an Alien UFO Easter Egg. This also unlocks The Truth Is Out There trophy & achievement.

The UFO can be found at the road north-west of Starlet Motel in Kelly County, specifically at night after 2:00 AM. You have to come solo. It’s slightly random and not a guaranteed spawn.

At the roadside you will sometimes find a blinking car. If the car is there you can trigger the UFO. If it’s not there, drive away and come back. Next to the blinking car, follow the path into the woods and you’ll see the UFO (bright blue light in sky). Approach it and the UFO will drop a woman it abducted.

Talk to her and offer to help. She asks you to go back to her car at the roadside. Do this and the UFO comes back to abduct you. Then you wake up 3 days later in the morning, with the indication being that they put an implant in your neck.
