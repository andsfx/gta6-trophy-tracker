# Actions Speak Louder Trophy in Grand Theft Auto VI

- #### Actions Speak Louder

 

Choices made (for better or worse).

## How to unlock Actions Speak Louder

In GTA VI, pretty much everything you do will contribute to your Criminal Profile, and you’ll know when a choice does, because you’ll get a little pop-up every time you do on the right-hand side of the screen – if you kill a civilian by running them over, you’ll get the little purple devil symbol pop-up, for instance.

There are various different pop-ups you can get, and they include:

- Professional Action – Yellow devil symbol – You’ll get these if you complete robberies without any casualties, make a clean getaway, make yourself incognito and more. Anything where you limit damage and chaos generally gives you this, like disabling an alarm before and destroying footage – of which you get 1 point for each.
- Hardened Action - Orange devil symbol – If you do end up in combat, you’ll get a Hardened pop-up if you don’t kill someone, but you incapacitate them – AKA shoot them in the knee.
- Violent Action - Purple devil symbol – If you kill a cop or a civilian needlessly, you’ll get a Violent Action pop-up. This can include shooting a fleeing civilian, running over people and more.
- Psycho Action- Light purple devil symbol with red eyes – This is an escalation of the previous action. If you kill lots of cops, murder lots of defenceless citizens, and kill vulnerable animals like cats and dogs, you’ll get this pop-up. Yes, even if you accidentally run them over! Eek!

So, to unlock the ‘Actions Speak Louder’ trophy, you need to get 500 of these pop-ups. It’s obviously a lot easier to get a headshot kill or run over a civilian and get a Violent Action pop-up than it is a Professional Action, but they all count.

You’ll likely unlock this by just going for 100%, especially with how many banks, stores and Illegal Operations you have to complete. If not, you can repeat the same Illegal Operations and bank jobs over and over again. Or, you can just plough through a load of cats. Actually, on second thought, don’t do that…
