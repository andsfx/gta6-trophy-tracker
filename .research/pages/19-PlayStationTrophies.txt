# Earn Cash, Not Kudos Trophy in Grand Theft Auto VI

- #### Earn Cash, Not Kudos

 

Win 20 different races.

## How to unlock Earn Cash, Not Kudos

GTA VI is absolutely chock-a-block with races. You have dirt road races, you have street races, you have destruction derbies, and you also have track races with a number of different disciplines like sports cars, super cars and motorbikes. If you love racing, GTA VI will definitely satisfy that itch!

There are 42 races in all, but for the ‘Earn Cash, Not Kudos’ trophy, you only need to win 20 of them. That said, you will need to win 30 for GTA VI’s 100% completion (see A Long Road ). My only tip here is, do not race fair. Your opposition won’t, so why should you?
