## How to unlock Challenging Tank

A Rhino in the GTA universe is, of course, one of the game’s tanks, and all you need to do for this trophy is jack one. Nice!

While it might sound tricky, because of how the new Wanted System works, it’s not as bad as it has been previously – it used to be an instant 6-star Wanted Level as soon as you entered the military base airspace or grounds. In GTA VI, we managed to get into the base before we triggered any major alarms.

So, first things first, the military base, known as Mile Zero Air Base, can be found to the far south of Leonida, in an area called Goose Key – to the west of Southwind, a small town. 

To get into the military base, you can fly through the gates in a car, use the Unique Stunt Jump to the southwest to get over the fence, or, you can use the easiest way, which is to use the helicopter on the helipad to the south of Southwind, where there's pretty much always a chopper there.

Then, you're going to want to head to the southeast of the large warehouses to the south of the base. There you'll find loads of Rhino tanks and APCs too – and not a lot of resistance if you're quick.

So, dive down in your chopper, land by the tanks, jump in a Rhino and start hightailing it out of there. Once you do, the ‘Challenging Tank’ trophy will unlock. Beware, though, that while you might not get any stars before getting to the tank, as soon as you get inside, you'll get a 4-star Wanted Level.

Let chaos proceed! Good luck getting it back to the city, though!!
