# GTA 6 All Meditation Spot Locations

by [PowerPyx](https://www.powerpyx.com/author/powerpyx/) [Leave a Comment](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-all-meditation-spot-locations/#respond)

GTA 6 (Grand Theft Auto VI) contains 18 Meditations across 10 Meditation Spot Locations. Completing any 15 of 18 Meditations is needed for the **[100% Completion Checklist](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-100-completion-checklist-guide/)**. Completing all Meditation Spots unlocks the **[Positive Mental Attitude trophy & achievement](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-grand-theft-auto-vi-trophy-guide-roadmap/).**

They get marked by a purple icon on the map when going near them. You can complete each spot with both characters to count it as 2 meditations. You can play as both characters after [Chapter 2 – Main Mission: Coffee Break](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-coffee-break-walkthrough/).

Progress can be tracked under Progress Menu > Collections > Exploration > Meditations. You can also filter for them under Map > Exploration (magnifying glass icon) > Meditate Around Leonida.

The first meditation is missable and can only be done in Chapter 1.

## All Meditation Locations

**Meditation Spot #1 (Meditation 1) – Jason’s House Meditation [MISSABLE – CHAPTER 1 EXCLUSIVE]** At some point during Chapter 1, Lucia will talk about meditating and walks out to the balcony. For me this happened in the morning after [Chapter 1 – Main Mission: Down to Earth (Meet Brian)](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-down-to-earth-walkthrough/), but the timing might vary. After talking to Lucia in the kitchen in the morning, she will say “You know what I’m gonna do? Meditate.” – she then goes to the balcony where the training equipment is. Follow her and you’ll get an optional quest popup in the top right corner (black background with pink door icon). Talk to Lucia on the balcony by looking at her with / and choose “Accept”. Then she shows you how to meditate.
