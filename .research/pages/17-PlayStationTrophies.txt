# The Shootist Trophy in Grand Theft Auto VI

- #### The Shootist

 

Earn a gold medal in all Shooting Range challenges.

## How to unlock The Shootist

One of the activities you can get involved in while wandering around Leonida is taking on some of the local Shooting Range challenges. There are two Shooting Ranges in Leonida:

- Rodgers Ranch & Shooting Range – This can be found to the northeast of Port Gellhorn, where you spend Chapter 2. There are 24 gold medals on offer here.
- Griffin Shooting Range – This one is on the outskirts of Vice City, in the heart of Leonida. On the northwest side of Vice City is a district called Balsero. The Griffin Shooting Range is in the north of Balsero. There are 18 gold medals on offer here.

So, to get ‘The Shootist’ trophy, you need to get 42 gold medals – you’ll need that many for GTA VI’s 100% completion anyway (see A Long Road ).

A few things of importance to note:

- Rodgers is a traditional target shooting range, while Griffin is a more of a run and gun range.
- It’s relatively cheap to get involved and you do get unlimited ammo while on the course, provided you use your own gun. You can also rent guns, though, but for an extra cost.
- There are a few courses where you’ll have to do two body shots and a headshot on the same target if you’re to reach the gold medal score. We mention that in the guide below.
- Each attempt, whether successful or not, will raise your Focus by 1%.
- The courses are always the same, so if you’re struggling, learn the patterns of what spawns and where.
