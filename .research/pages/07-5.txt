# Grand Theft Auto VI Trophy Guide & Road Map

Grand Theft Auto VI Trophy Guide & Road Map

### Road Map

Overview:

- Estimated trophy difficulty: 7/10 (only because the drone time trials and races actually require a decent amount of skill and patience) (Platinum Difficulty Poll)
- Offline trophies: 55 (46, 6, 2, 1)
- Online trophies: 0
- Approximate amount of time to platinum: 200+ hours, easily (Estimated Time to Platinum)
- Minimum number of playthroughs: 1 (there’s no mission replay anymore, but you will get thrown back into the open-world after the final credits, to just before the last mission)
- Number of missable trophies: Potentially 1 (Positive Mental Attitude )
- Glitched trophies: 0
- Does difficulty affect trophies?: No, there are no difficulty settings

 Introduction: GTA VI is a Bonnie and Clyde-esque destructive romp through Leonida, Rockstar’s take on Florida, with Vice City like you’ve never seen it before. With a massive campaign of 90+ missions, that probably equates to a 60 hour completion just for that alone - provided you don’t veer off the beaten path - it’s a monster of a game. That’s the thing, though, if you don’t step off the beaten path, you’re missing so much. So much that makes GTA VI such a wonderful experience.

It only took 13 years, folks, but it’s finally here! Was it worth the wait? Absolutely! GTA 6 is a generational masterpiece, with a truly organic open-world that blurs the line between the game’s story mission and its emergent gameplay. The world is huge and so full of mystery, that players will no doubt be discovering new things for the months to come. 

Walkthrough: So, let’s talk trophies. It should come as no surprise that as the map gets bigger (2-3 times the size of Red Dead Redemption 2) and the story gets bigger, that the trophy list gets more time consuming too. And boy is it time consuming.

It’ll probably take you 60-70 hours to romp through the campaign, and then you pretty much need to do everything else after that. Yes, there is a 100% trophy (as expected), and that’s likely going to take 200+ hours to get there, as you pretty much have to do everything.
