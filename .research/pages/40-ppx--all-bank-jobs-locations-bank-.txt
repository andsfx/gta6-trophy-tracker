TITLE: GTA 6 All Bank Jobs Locations (Bank Vaults)
SITE: PowerPyx

GTA 6 (Grand Theft Auto VI) contains 22 Bank Job Locations (14 Small Banks & 8 Large Bank Vaults). Fully completing all Bank Jobs is required for the **100% Completion Checklist**. You also need to rob 5 Large Bank Vaults for the **Maximum Withdrawal** trophy & achievement.

**Small Banks** are unlocked after Chapter 3 – Main Mission: Credit Check (Meet Raul’s Crew). That’s halfway through the story. This mission unlocks the Dye Pack Jammer which you need to fence marked bills from banks. When unfogging the map the banks then get marked automatically with a green bank icon . You can use the map filter to see how many you’ve found: Map &gt; Robberies (Money Bag icon) &gt; Bank Jobs. Completed ones will have a bright green icon and incomplete ones will have a dark green icon. Small Banks always have a Cash Drawer and Safe which you must both loot to count the Bank Job as “completed”. You can scroll over each map icon and it will show which objectives you completed.

**Large Banks (Vaults)** are unlocked after Chapter 4 – Optional Mission: That’s Where The Money Is… (Wait at the Cafe). This spawns at the very end of Chapter 4, near the end of the story. This is a sort of endgame feature and by far the best way to make money. You can steal up to $205,000 per bank within a few minutes. The mission will walk you through how bank jobs work. You must follow security vans (armored trucks) and wait for them to stop at a bank. A security guard will carry a briefcase with cash into the vault, then the vault is temporarily open. Break in and steal the cash in the vault. Large Banks always have a Vault, Cash Drawer, Safe. After this quest you can pay Yasmani (the fence unlocked from story in Chapter 3) for tips about security vans. If you haven’t done Yasmani’s previous quests yet, you must do those first (robbing 2 Payday Loans), then he sells tips about security vans for $10,000. Robbing bank vaults instantly gives a 4-star wanted level. Use a fast motorcycle to cut through traffic to get away from the cops. Then switch to another vehicle to become unrecognizable.
