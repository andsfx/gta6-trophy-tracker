# GTA 6 The Cartel Heist Walkthrough

by [Gage](https://www.powerpyx.com/author/gage/) [Leave a Comment](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-the-cartel-heist-walkthrough/#respond)

**The Cartel Heist** is the 74th main mission in GTA 6 (Grand Theft Auto VI). This walkthrough will guide you through all objectives of **The Cartel Heist** main quest.

* **Starting Location:** Vice City > Stockyard
* **Chapter:** Chapter 3
* **Quest Giver:** Raul
* **Requirement:** Complete “Hidden Charges” and all other Chapter 3 main missions
* **Can Be Started By:** Lucia/Jason
* **Reward:** $120,000, ![:bronze:](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-includes/images/smilies/bronze.png) Break the Bank trophy/achievement
* **Quest Description:** With their preparations made, Raul’s crew hits the Sinfrontera National Bank. Some time later, Raul calls and says he’ll send an advance on their earnings and that he has a suite in the Y Hotel they can move into.

1. Get in the car
2. Drive toward the cell towers
3. Park the vehicle
4. Go to the bank
5. Enter the bank
6. Wait for Karim’s signal
7. Knock out a security guard
8. Enter the elevator
9. Intimidate the security guard
10. Confront La Polilla Negra Security
11. Take the elevator to the roof
12. Secure the backup terminal
13. Open the vault
14. Loot the vault
15. Hold off the cops
16. Regroup with Raul
17. Deal with the SWAT team
18. Search the SWAT truck
19. Get to the bus
20. Help Nicole
21. Find a way down to the basement
22. Go to the delivery bay
23. Leave the bank
24. Steal the Brigade
25. Escape the area
26. Defend the bus
27. Push back the helicopter
28. Catch up to the bus
29. Pull alongside the bus
30. Escape on the freeway

## Starting Location: The Cartel Heist

After completing “Hidden Charges” and all other Chapter 3 main missions, Raul will contact you to meet up at the planning room to start the heist.

[![](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-content/uploads/gta6-walkthrough-the-cartel-heist-1-300x169.jpg)](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-content/uploads/gta6-walkthrough-the-cartel-heist-1.jpg) [![](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-content/uploads/gta6-walkthrough-the-cartel-heist-2-300x169.jpg)](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-content/uploads/gta6-walkthrough-the-cartel-heist-2.jpg)

## Get in the car

After the initial scene, go outside and get in the waiting black car with Lucia and Nicole.

## Drive toward the cell towers

Then start driving toward downtown to where you placed the bombs on the cell towers previously.
