# GTA 6 All Collectible Card Sets Locations

by [Darth\_Krid](https://www.powerpyx.com/author/darth_krid/) [Leave a Comment](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-all-collectible-card-sets-locations/#respond)

GTA 6 (Grand Theft Auto VI) includes 18 Collectible Card Sets, which contain a total of 480 unique cards. Collecting at least 3 complete sets is required for the trophy/achievement **Don’t Touch My Deck**, whereas collecting at least 15 complete sets is required for the **[100% Completion Checklist](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-100-completion-checklist-guide/)**and trophy/achievement **A Long Road**.

Collectible Cards are found all throughout the game world, inside and outside buildings. Cards inside buildings are typically found on surfaces or stuck to walls, whereas cards found outside buildings are typically found in yellow phone boxes on walls or tool boxes on surfaces. Unlike in Red Dead Redemption 2, cards cannot be farmed by purchasing items, they can only be obtained by picking them up, so there is a finite number of cards. Completing 15 sets requires picking up at minimum 372 out of 480 unique cards depending on which sets you complete, as different sets have different amounts of cards available.

After card is obtained it can then be sold at any of the Pawn Shops present in the game, for differing values depending on the “rarity” of the card, ranging from $20 to upwards of $1000+. Selling cards doesn’t remove them from your Collections list, so you don’t need to purposely keep found cards if you would rather sell them for money before a set is complete.

Cards within a set can have different color versions of the same card (Bronze/Silver/Gold, Red/Blue/Purple). Collecting each color version counts as a unique card for the completion of the set. Some cards can be found multiple times in different locations, but this does not count multiple times toward set completion, it only gives a duplicate of the same card that you can sell again.
