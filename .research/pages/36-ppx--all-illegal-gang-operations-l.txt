# GTA 6 All Illegal Gang Operations Locations

by [PowerPyx](https://www.powerpyx.com/author/powerpyx/) [Leave a Comment](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-all-illegal-gang-operations-locations/#respond)

GTA 6 (Grand Theft Auto VI) contains 39 Illegal Gang Operation Locations. Finding and looting all Gang Operations is required for the **[100% Completion Checklist](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-100-completion-checklist-guide/)** and the trophy / achievement **Pest Control** (rob 1 illegal operation of each gang) and **A Long Road** (attain 100% completion).

They get marked on the map with a yellow icon  when entering their location. You can use the map filter to see how many you’ve found: Map > Robberies (Money Bag icon) > Illegal Operations. The ones you visited will show their unique name and the ones you haven’t visited are called “Robbery Opportunity”. You can also keep track under Progress Menu > Gangs > it shows how many Operations you discovered and completed of each gang.

When you scroll over their map icon, it shows how many key valuables they contain. These are usually a few collectibles such as unique weapon variants, luxury watches, and a safe with cash inside. Make sure you grab all key valuables to count the location as fully completed. After completing an operation it gets grayed out for a while, after enough time passes it respawns and can be completed again. They are also a great way to make money, they often contain narcotics that you can sell to a [fence](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-all-fence-locations-how-to-fence-stolen-goods/).

All Gang Operations are listed here in the same order as the Map & Activities menu so you can easily compare what you’re missing.
