# GTA 6 Full Walkthrough – All Missions

by [PowerPyx](https://www.powerpyx.com/author/powerpyx/) [Leave a Comment](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-full-walkthrough-all-missions/#respond)

Here you can find a full GTA 6 (Grand Theft Auto VI) Walkthrough of all Main Story Missions, Optional Missions, Side Tasks, and Strangers & Freaks Side Quests.

The story consists of 5 chapters and takes 60-80 hours. Some optional missions are time-sensitive, they will show an hourglass icon on the map. Do them as soon as they appear or they will be missed. This doesn’t affect trophies or [100% Completion](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-100-completion-checklist-guide/) however.

GTA VI doesn’t feature mission gold medals and there’s no mission replay. It’s good to keep a manual save at the start and end of each chapter, in case you want to replay anything later.

You can still do all Strangers & Freaks questlines after the story in free-roam.

Main Missions appear as a pink icon with black letters, they are the only relevant quests for story progression. Optional side missions have black icon with pink letters. Smaller side tasks have a purple icon.

Mission names appear briefly as pink text in the bottom right corner of the screen at the start of quests. Their starting name on the map is labelled differently than the actual quest name after starting it, for easier referencing both names will be listed here. Objectives appear briefly at the bottom of the screen as pink text. Missions can appear in slightly different order. You will get phone messages from the quest givers which marks their quests. It doesn’t matter in what order you play them, you always get their quests or later. If a quest doesn’t appear, go do other quests or try sleeping.

You can view a list of available missions by opening the map and clicking the pink story book icon. This opens a dropdown menu with all pending missions. You can view completed main missions under Progress > Story Recap, but it’s inconsistent which missions it lists there and which it does not. Completed optional missions aren’t listed anywhere.
