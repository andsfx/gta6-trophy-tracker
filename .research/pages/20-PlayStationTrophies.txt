# Baited My Tackle Trophy in Grand Theft Auto VI

- #### Baited My Tackle

 

Beat Cal in a fishing challenge.

## How to unlock Baited My Tackle

Fairly early on in the game, Cal and Jason will get super competitive with their fishing. It won’t take long until Cal texts you and says “Check it out! 2lbs! Parrotfish out the wazoo right here. Beat that!” So, you have to beat that, basically.

The game will drop a pin in the map, which leads to the southwest side of Gambit Bay (to the west of Key Lento). Head there in a boat with your fishing rod in hand, and head to the west side of the large purple circle. There you’ll find a load of Stoplight Parrotfish. Catch one bigger than 2lbs, and then send the photo to Cal. Once you do, you’ll win the fishing challenge and the ‘Baited My Tackle’ trophy will unlock.

As an aside, you don’t have to do this early, either, as we didn’t do it until well after we’d finished the campaign.
