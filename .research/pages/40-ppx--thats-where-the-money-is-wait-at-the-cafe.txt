# GTA 6 That’s Where The Money Is… (Wait at the Cafe) Walkthrough

by [Gage](https://www.powerpyx.com/author/gage/) [Leave a Comment](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-thats-where-the-money-is-wait-at-the-cafe-walkthrough/#respond)

**That’s Where The Money Is… (Wait at the Cafe)** is an optional main mission in GTA 6 (Grand Theft Auto VI). This walkthrough will guide you through all objectives of **That’s Where The Money Is… (Wait at the Cafe)** main quest.

* **Starting Location:** Vice Beach > Ocean Beach
* **Chapter:** 4
* **Quest Giver:** Yasmani
* **Requirement:** Complete Chapter 4 “VCIA Takedown”
* **Can Be Started By:** Lucia/Jason
* **Reward:** Yasmani sells Security Van tips + around $80,000 (if you grabbed all Marked Bills) + ![:bronze:](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-includes/images/smilies/bronze.png) A Classic Blitz Play trophy/achievement (if you hadn’t earned it previously)
* **Quest Description:** Yasmani gave an opportunity to hit a bank vault. Wait for the security van at Cafe Orquidea and tail it to the bank.

**Objectives:**

1. Tail the security van
2. Tail the guard
3. Rob the vault
4. Lose the cops

## Starting Location: That’s Where The Money Is… (Wait at the Cafe)

After completing “VCIA Takedown”, Yasmani will contact you with an opportunity, which is the first time you can try robbing a bank vault on your own from a tip from him. Head inside the cafe and sit at the seats against the window.

## Tail the security van

After the van drives by, quickly jump in your vehicle and follow behind it. Stay a bit of distance away so you don’t scare the driver.

## Tail the guard

The truck will stop outside the bank. Park nearby and then follow the guard inside.

## Rob the vault

Inside you can put on a disguise and then pull your gun to start robbing the bank. Go to the back of the room where there is a doorway that you can plant explosives on to open.

Watch out for the guards that will be in the vault, and deal with them to continue.

Head into the vault and start grabbing the pile of marked bills in the corner on the cart.
