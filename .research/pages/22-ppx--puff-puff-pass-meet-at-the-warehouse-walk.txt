# GTA 6 Puff Puff Pass (Meet at the Warehouse) Walkthrough

by [PowerPyx](https://www.powerpyx.com/author/powerpyx/) [Leave a Comment](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-puff-puff-pass-meet-at-the-warehouse-walkthrough/#respond)

**Puff Puff Pass (Meet at the Warehouse)** is a Strangers and Freaks Mission (Side Quest) in GTA 6 (Grand Theft Auto VI). This walkthrough will guide you through all objectives of the **Puff Puff Pass (Meet at the Warehouse)** side mission.

* **Starting Location:** Leonard County > Cecilia’s Mansion
* **Chapter:** 3
* **Quest Giver:** Andy / Cecilia
* **Requirement:** Finished [Read the Label (Retrieve the Cellerica Van)](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-read-the-label-retrieve-the-cellerica-van-walkthrough/) and wait for Cecilia to reach out (must play as Lucia).
* **Can Be Started By:** Lucia only
* **Reward:** $10,000, **![trophy](//www.powerpyx.com/wp-content/uploads/trophy.png "Bronze Trophy")****Road to Rehabilitation** trophy/achievement
* **Quest Description:** Cellerica’s leader, Cecilia, needs help cleaning up a problem. Meet her at the Paper Boys warehouse.
* **Questline (Andy):**[Climbing the Cellerichain (Part 1-3)](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-climbing-the-cellerichain-walkthrough/) > [Pay the Cellerica Fee](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-pay-the-cellerica-fee-walkthrough/) > [Peach! (Join the Cellerica Initiation)](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-peach-join-the-cellerica-initiation-walkthrough/) > [Read the Label (Retrieve the Cellerica Van)](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-read-the-label-retrieve-the-cellerica-van-walkthrough/) > **[Puff Puff Pass (Meet at the Warehouse)](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-puff-puff-pass-meet-at-the-warehouse-walkthrough/)**

**Objectives:**

1. Follow Felix
2. Enter the room
3. Escape the warehouse
4. Go to Cecilia’s mansion
5. Find Cecilia

## Starting Location: Puff Puff Pass (Meet at the Warehouse)

After Andy’s 4th quest [Read the Label (Retrieve the Cellerica Van)](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-read-the-label-retrieve-the-cellerica-van-walkthrough/), play as Lucia and do other tasks until Cecilia reaches out again and wants you to come to the warehouse in Belville. This takes a while to trigger.
