TITLE: GTA 6 - T**s Ass Drug Money Trophy - Complete Chapter 2 by Josie
SITE: Mobalytics

To get the **T\*\*s Ass Drug Money** Trophy, you must complete Chapter 2 of the story, the trophy is unmissable with natural progression.

Still need help with other trophies? Check out the main page:

Once you have completed all of the main objectives in Chapter 2, you will be called to head back to the Motel, once you enter the bathroom of the Motel Room will show VC, to progress to Vice City.

After opening the bathroom door, there will be a series of cutscenes, once these are done, you will have to make your way through the car park of the Motel, taking out all of the enemies here.

Once these are all defeated, make your way to Lucia in the Motel Diner, from here you will have to continue defending yourself.

After taking out all the enemies, you will exit the Motel Diner and separately take vehicles, Jason will be in a car, Lucia on a bike. As Lucia, proceed to follow Jason during the chase scene.

After following Jason, you will need to jump off of the bike and into the car, after this, there will be a further chase scene where as Lucia, you will be shooting at enemy cars.

After this, there will be cutscenes, and you will have made it into Vice City in the dark.

On the drive, Jason will call Dre'Quan, he will give you directions on how to get to his club. Once you arrive, you will need to enter in through the back entrance, there will be a cutscene in meeting up with Dre'Quan.

Once inside, you can choose to take or decline the cash for the strippers, but you will need to make your way upstairs to continue.

Finish everything in the club and after the cutscenes, you will be back outside, head back around to your car to make it to the new apartment.

Once you make it to the apartment, head inside for the final cutscene, once Jason and Lucia wake up the following morning, the T\*\*s Ass Drugs Money Trophy will pop.
