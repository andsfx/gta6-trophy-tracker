# Fair Game Trophy in Grand Theft Auto VI

- #### Fair Game

 

Hunt a unique animal for Bret.

## How to unlock Fair Game

Bret is Jason's old army buddy, and he'll get in touch with you when you're in Chapter 3. He'll want you to go and visit him at his place way up in the north of Leonida. There he runs his successful hunting business, and he'll get you set up to go out hunting.

A short while into completing his “Target Decks” you’ll get a message about a Snapmatic Animal – AKA a legendary animal. The first one you receive is the Snapmatic Buck, and the text reads…

“Hey Duval. So I took a handful of mushrooms a few nights back and went for a run around Grayson. Saw some crazy stuff that might not have been real, but I also saw this deer that had huge ass antlers and I think that might’ve been real. Anyway if you’re ever northeast of Grayson, between the freeway and the ocean keep an eye out for it. A deer like that has to have some crazy hormones I can use.”

So, head to the place Bret describes (Grayson is in the northwest of the map) and look for the Snapmatic Buck at night. Once you kill it and get Bret to pick it up, you’ll unlock the ‘Fair Game’ trophy.
