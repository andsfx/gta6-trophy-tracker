# GTA 6 All Payday Loans Jobs Locations

by [PowerPyx](https://www.powerpyx.com/author/powerpyx/) [Leave a Comment](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-all-payday-loans-jobs-locations/#respond)

GTA 6 (Grand Theft Auto VI) contains 7 Payday Loans Job Locations. Fully completing all Payday Loans is required for the **[100% Completion Checklist](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-100-completion-checklist-guide/)**and it’s one of the requirements for [**A Long Road** trophy & achievement](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-grand-theft-auto-vi-trophy-guide-roadmap/).

**Payday Loans** are unlocked after [Chapter 3 – Main Mission: Credit Check (Meet Raul’s Crew)](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-credit-check-meet-rauls-crew-walkthrough/). That’s halfway through the story. This mission unlocks the Dye Pack Jammer which you need to fence marked bills from Payday Loans and [Banks](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-all-bank-jobs-locations-bank-vaults/). When unfogging the map the Payday Loans then get marked automatically with a green hand icon ![](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-content/uploads/gta-6-payday-loans.png). You can use the map filter to see how many you’ve found: Map > Robberies (Money Bag icon) > Payday Loans. Completed ones will have a bright green icon and incomplete ones will have a dark green icon. Aim a gun to threaten the teller, let him put the cash on the counter, steal it, also enter the backroom and rob the safe, then escape the cops.
