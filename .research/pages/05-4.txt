# GTA 6 - Retail Therapy Trophy Guide / Achievement Guide

GTA 6 (Grand Theft Auto VI) Retail Therapy Trophy Guide / Achievement Guide - Rob 3 shops back-to-back with your partner without losing your Wanted Level. [Bronze]

You can only start doing this after Chapter 2 - Main Mission: Coffee Break, that's when you can switch between both characters. Simply hit 3 robbery targets (green icons on map) while being chased by police the entire time. You'll want to maintain the red/blue flashing wanted level (not the red heat level). Equip an armored vest and sticky bombs (bought at Ammu-Nations) and Zombix/Adrenaline for healing. Call your partner. Then loot 3 shops in a row. If it doesn't unlock after the 3rd shop, hit a 4th shop and keep going until it unlocks.

The trophy unlock is delayed, it pops shortly after leaving the 3rd shop and driving away.

To make sure it counts, you'll want to rob a couple items in each store and ideally go into the back room and break the safe with sticky bombs. If you leave the first store before police arrive, you may need to hit a 4th store.
