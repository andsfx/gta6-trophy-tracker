GTA 6 (Grand Theft Auto VI) Catch Me If You Can Trophy Guide / Achievement Guide - Make an unscheduled departure at VCIA. [Bronze]

This is unlocked by stealing one of the large passenger planes that's docked to one of the departure terminals at VCIA (Vice City International Airport). It doesn't work with the passenger planes on the airfield, has to be one that's connected to one of the passenger boarding bridges. Inside the plane go upstairs, enter the cockpit, drive the plane backward to the airstrip, take off and fly far enough away from the airport until the trophy unlocks.

The easiest plane is the one connected to departure terminals E3/E4. Drive through one of the fences around the airfield and head straight to the plane. Next to the boarding bridge are stairs with a door leading directly into the plane.

The trophy unlock is a bit delayed, you must fly 1km (0.6 miles) away from the airport. You can also enter the plane by going through the airport terminal on foot, you'll find it docked to departure terminal E3/E4. Going over the airfield is faster though.
