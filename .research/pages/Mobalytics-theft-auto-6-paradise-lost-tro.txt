## Overview

To get the Paradise Lost Trophy, you must complete Chapter 1 of the story, the trophy is unmissable with natural progression.

**Important to note:** During Chapter 1, there are a few things that you must make sure of before making it to Chapter 2 as there will be a point of no return at the end of the chapter.

**Missable:** Make sure that you are taking part in the meditation with Lucia in Chapter 1 at Jason's place, she will talk about wanting to clear her head, make sure that you join her for this Meditation spot.

There is also the opportunity to launch all three games on the JoySystem when playing it as part of the story with Cal at Jason's place, 2 games launch automatically, but launching the third on the console has to be done manually. If you don't do this here, you will have to launch the game later on, I will remind you of this again in Chapter 3.

## Paradise Lost Trophy

Progress all the way through Chapter 1, until you are walking into the hospital as Lucia.

Once you make it to the hospital, head inside and go left and up the stairs to the first floor.

Once you make it to the next Reception desk, head through the white double doors at the back.

Through here, talk with one of the nurses and ask for "John Doe", she will direct you to the room where Jason is.

After the cutscene, Lucia will take Jason out of the room in a wheelchair, you will be stopped by one of the nurses, but you need to keep an eye on the guy coming through the white double doors, be ready to take him out as Jason. (He will remain in the wheelchair)

Once he is dealt with, Lucia will not stop walking so be ready for the upcoming enemies and take these out also, she will wheel Jason into an elevator and you will take this together.

After taking the elevator, Lucia will keep walking again, you will have to be quick in taking out the enemies along the way.

Upcoming, you will get jumped by an enemy and will have to defend yourself as Lucia.

After the cutscene, Lucia will be dragging Jason, and you will need to take out the enemies through the glass as Jason to protect both he and Lucia.

Keep progressing until Jason is back in the wheelchair and you have to take on more enemies throughout the hospital.

Keep progressing until you switch back to Lucia and start shooting at all the enemies in the darker hospital area, you will need to defend Jason here.

Keep going until you make it into the colourful corridor area, here, be careful not to shoot innocent civilians, only take on the enemies.

Keep progressing through the hospital remembering not to shoot the innocent.

After the next short cutscene, Lucia and Jason will split up, Lucia will go to find a ride out of there, meanwhile Jason is still in the wheelchair. He will get jumped by an enemy, you will have to defend yourself here. Keep doing so until Lucia brings the ride to escape.

The next segment will be a chase scene, where you are shooting the enemies chasing you as Jason from the back of the Ambulance truck.

The chase sequence is over once you make it over the railway and the oncoming train stops the enemies from chasing you further.

There will be many cutscenes upcoming so keep on enjoying the story, you will then wake up as Jason in a Motel room, Lucia will come over and as her, you will both be looking through the contents of the case you have.

Once you have looked over everything, you will have control of Lucia in the Motel room, and here is where the Paradise Lost Trophy will pop, signifying the completion of Chapter 1.
