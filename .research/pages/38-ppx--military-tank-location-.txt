# GTA 6 Military Tank Location

by [PowerPyx](https://www.powerpyx.com/author/powerpyx/) [Leave a Comment](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-military-tank-location/#respond)

In GTA 6 (Grand Theft Auto VI), you can find military tanks at Mile Zero Air Station. It’s the island in the south-west edge of the map in Mariana County. The tanks stand in the south of the air base.

Just drive through the gate or do the stunt jump next to the south-east entrance to jump over the fence. The tanks are unlocked and you can simply enter them.

[![](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-content/uploads/gta-6-challenging-tank-1-300x169.jpg)](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-content/uploads/gta-6-challenging-tank-1.jpg) [![](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-content/uploads/gta-6-challenging-tank-2-300x169.jpg)](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-content/uploads/gta-6-challenging-tank-2.jpg)

[![](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-content/uploads/gta-6-challenging-tank-3-300x169.jpg)](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-content/uploads/gta-6-challenging-tank-3.jpg) [![](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-content/uploads/gta-6-challenging-tank-4-300x169.jpg)](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-content/uploads/gta-6-challenging-tank-4.jpg)

Taking a tank will instantly give you a 3-star wanted level. Tanks can’t be saved as personal vehicles to the garage because they’re too big to fit in the garage.

Stealing a tank also unlocks the **![trophy](//www.powerpyx.com/wp-content/uploads/trophy.png "Bronze Trophy")**[**Challenging Tank trophy & achievement**](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-grand-theft-auto-vi-trophy-guide-roadmap/).
