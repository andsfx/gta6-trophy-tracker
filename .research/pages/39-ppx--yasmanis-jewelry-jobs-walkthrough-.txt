**Yasmani’s Jewelry Jobs** is the 53rd main mission in GTA 6 (Grand Theft Auto VI). This walkthrough will guide you through all objectives of the **Yasmani’s Jewelry Jobs** main quest.

- **Starting Location:** Vice City/Ocean Beach Banks
- **Chapter:** Chapter 3
- **Quest Giver:** Yasmani
- **Requirement:** Complete Chapter 3 “Boobie Time“
- **Can Be Started By:** Lucia/Jason
- **Reward:** Snap Gun (unlock doors without triggering alarms) + Yasmani pickup from Garage drop-offs + $20,000 from objective items + extra dependent on how much you steal from each location
- **Quest Description:** Yasmani is after items that are stored in safes at these banks.

**Objectives:**

1. Hit Casa de Empeno
2. Hit Richardson Jewelry
3. Hit Donovan Banks & Co.

## Starting Location: Yasmani’s Jewelry Jobs

After completing “Boobie Time” Yasmani will contact you about some jobs robbing specific jewelry stores to obtain specific items he wants.

You can do these in whichever order that you want, but it is recommended to deliver the jewelry to Yasmani after doing the first one because he will then give you the **Snap Gun** which allows you to open doors without triggering alarms, which makes the other robberies much easier as police will take longer to arrive.

## Hit Casa De Empeno

“Yasmani is after a buffalo skull chain that’s stored in a safe here.”

This jewelry store is recommended to be done first, because there isn’t a back door that you can enter with the Snap Gun. It is the smallest of the stores which makes getting in and out while triggering the alarm much quicker.

In this jewelry store the safe is in the back small office. You can also interact with the security box to the left of the safe to Destroy Video footage, which prevents police from having your description if you escape the area before they arrive.

Interact with the safe and enter the code **1680** to open it. Loot every inside to obtain the buffalo skull chain that Yasmani is requesting.

You can also smash the display cases in the store to grab more jewelry to sell.

## Hit Richardson Jewelry

“Yasmani is after a diamond ring that’s stored in a safe here.”

This jewelry store has a back door which you can open with the Snap Gun, allowing you to enter the back room without alerting security in the front of the store.

You can Disarm Security with the panel to the right of the door leading to the center room.

You can Destroy Foot on the box under the security screen in the central room.

The safe is in the northeast corner of the central room. Enter the code **6815** to open the safe and loot the items inside.

If you came in quietly you choose to leave right now to avoid alerting anyone, but if you would rather steal more for extra money you can go through the door to the front of the store and steal the items out of the cases.

## Hit Donovan Banks & Co.

“Yasmani is after an amethyst necklace that’s stored in a safe here.”

This jewelry store has a back door which you can open with the Snap Gun, allowing you to enter the back room without alerting security in the front of the store.

You can Destroy Footage and Disarm Security in the small office in the back.

The safe is in the middle of the long hallway room in the back. Enter code **0104** to open the safe and loot the items inside.

If you came in quietly you choose to leave right now to avoid alerting anyone, but if you would rather steal more for extra money you can go through the door to the front of the store and steal the items out of the cases.

After you have looted all items from all locations return to Yasmani at his store to sell the items and complete the mission.

This finishes Yasmani’s Jewelry Jobs in GTA VI.
