# Road to Rehabilitation Trophy in Grand Theft Auto VI

- #### Road to Rehabilitation

 

Complete the final mission for Andy.

## How to unlock Road to Rehabilitation

Around the time of the “Top Billing” main mission, the Dimez concert mission (Chapter 3, mission 11), Lucia will get a phone call from Andy, her long-time friend who you met in the Rusty Anchor in Chapter 1. 

Andy has a new life, and a get rich quick scheme that she wants you to help with. There are 5 missions that you need to complete to finish Andy’s mission strand. They are as follows:

- “Climbing the Cellerichain” (whereby you make multiple deliveries)
- Pay the $5,000 fee
- “Peach!”
- “Read the Label”
- “Puff Puff Pass”

Once you’ve finished “Puff Puff Pass,” you’ll unlock the ‘Road to Rehabilitation’ trophy, and Andy’s mission strand will come to a close.
