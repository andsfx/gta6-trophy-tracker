# Master of None Trophy in Grand Theft Auto VI

- #### Master of None

 

Try each type of activity once.

## How to unlock Master of None

There are a ton of activities to get involved in when playing GTA VI – as expected. And for trying every one of them once, you’ll unlock the ‘Master of None’ trophy. First things first, according to the trophy there are only 9 activities in GTA VI, although the game bundles a load of them together, like all the racing categories (which are clearly 3 or so, but they’ve made them 1). Secondly, they’ve just not included some activities like Meditation Spots, which is bizarre. 

So, you need to try each of the below activities (there is a caveat below, however). The 9 tracked activities are:

- Fishing
- Hunting
- Golf
- Basketball
- Pool
- Racing (whether it’s traditional racing, Race Challenges or Top Fun Drone time trials)
- Shooting Ranges
- Video Games
- Base Jump

Just as an FYI too, for instance, you actually have to complete the Base Jump, and get within Par Time – we did multiple Base Jumps, multiple times across multiple places, but the trophy only unlocked when we hit the Par Time on one of them. The same might be said for the other activities too, so if you’ve done them all, make sure you win/beat Par Time/beat an opponent if it doesn’t unlock.
