# GTA 6 trophy guide: Full list of trophies and achievements

Grand Theft Achievement.

At long last, GTA 6 is here, and that means there's a whole load of trophies and achievements for you to unlock.

It's going to take you plenty of time to complete the story, but if you want 100% completion, you're going to be in Vice City for quite a while.

So, to help you keep track, here's a full list of all the trophies and achievements in GTA 6 so you know just how far you are from that elusive Platinum.

## **GTA 6 trophy guide: Full list of trophies and achievements**

There are 55 total trophies to earn in GTA 6, which consists of one Platinum, two Gold, six Silver and 46 Bronze trophies.

Here is a full list of all the trophies and achievements in GTA 6:

- **Vice Got a Price!** - Congratulations! You're a Leonida Legend. (Platinum) - collect all other trophies
- **A Classic Blitz Play** - Rob an Armored Truck and escape. (Bronze)
- **A Long Road** - Attain 100% Completion. (Gold)
- **Actions Speak Louder** - Choices made (for better or worse). (Bronze)
- **Bad Luck, I Guess** - Second time's the charm. (Bronze)
- **Baited My Tackle** - Beat Cal in a fishing challenge. (Bronze)
- **Break the Bank** - Complete The Cartel Heist. (Bronze)
- **Capital Offense** - Smash your way out of a vehicle showroom in classic style. (Bronze)
- **Catch Me If You Can** - Make an unscheduled departure at VCIA. (Bronze)
- **Challenging Tank** - Kinda hot in these Rhinos. (Bronze)
- **Cunning Stunts** - Complete 250 Stunts. (Silver)

- **Earn Cash, Not Kudos** - Win 20 different races. (Bronze)

- **Family Matters** - Complete Child's Play. (Bronze)

- **Hi-Jacked** - Complete VCIA Takedown. (Bronze)
