# GTA 6 Spin On This (Shut Down the Labs) Walkthrough

by [Gage](https://www.powerpyx.com/author/gage/) [Leave a Comment](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-spin-on-this-shut-down-the-labs-walkthrough/#respond)

Spin On This (Shut Down the Labs) is an Optional Mission (Side Mission) in GTA 6 (Grand Theft Auto VI). This walkthrough will guide you through all objectives of the Spin On This (Shut Down the Labs) side mission.

* **Starting Location:** Lummox County > Grayson
* **Quest Giver:** Tyrone
* **Requirement:** Destroy enough Kitty Litter Labs
* **Can Be Started By:**Jason
* **Reward:** $10,000, Spin On This! trophy/achievement
* **Quest Description:** Tyrone wants the main synthetics operation shut down for good.
* **Questline (Tyrone):**Here, Kitty Kitty > Kitty Litter Labs > Spin On This (Shut Down the Labs)

1. Go to the motel
2. Find the synthetics lab in the motel
3. Get back to Tyrone/Loot the clerk
4. Break into the lab
5. Clear out the lab
6. Plant the explosives on the vats
7. Clear out the lab
8. Regroup with Tyrone
9. Chase Rocky
10. Regroup with Tyrone

## Starting Location: Spin On This (Shut Down the Labs)

To start the mission approach the diner at the marked location outside the town of Grayson. Note that you get the car with Tyrone and Dook automatically after approaching the area, so if you want to equip body armor and a longarm do this before approaching the diner on foot or in a vehicle.

## Go to the motel

You will have a motel marked to the east, follow the path to it.

## Find the synthetics lab in the motel

Tyrone enters the main motel room to distract the clerk, you need to find the entrance to the lab. Go down the stairs on the left to find a green door, with someone standing outside. If you want to try being stealthy, take out this person with a grab or weapon melee or they will start shooting at you when you try to enter the building.

Inside this room investigate the large wooden door on the floor.

## Get back to Tyrone/Loot the clerk

If you stayed in stealth you’ll be prompted to head back to Tyrone first, but if you started shooting or were shot at he’ll take out the clerk upstairs. Go through the door in front through the kitchen area and up the stairs to the entrance area, then you will need to loot the clerk to obtain the key to the door in the ground.

## Break into the lab

Go back downstairs while watching for any other enemies that might run in, then interact with the door in the ground to open it and access the lab.
