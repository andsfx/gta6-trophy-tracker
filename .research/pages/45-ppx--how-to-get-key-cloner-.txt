# GTA 6 How to Get Key Cloner

by [PowerPyx](https://www.powerpyx.com/author/powerpyx/) [Leave a Comment](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-how-to-get-key-cloner/#respond)

The Key Cloner allows you to hack into high-end cars in GTA 6 (Grand Theft Auto VI). It’s an automatic story unlock that requires significant story progress.

You get the Key Cloner automatically in Chapter 3 – [Main Mission: Clonin’ Keys, Makin’ GS (Steal the Zorrusso for Randy)](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-clonin-keys-makin-gs-steal-the-zorrusso-for-randy-walkthrough/). This is roughly halfway through Chapter 3 and around 40 hours into the game. At the start of this mission, you automatically get the Key Cloner. It gets installed to your phone settings.

Supercars that require the Key Cloner often have Advanced Trackers. Advanced Trackers will automatically alert the police when you slow down in a stolen car. If you don’t want to deal with this, look for cars that have Standard Trackers or no trackers at all.

You can see if a car requires the Key Cloner by scanning it with the Waink App (hold or select the app from phone).
