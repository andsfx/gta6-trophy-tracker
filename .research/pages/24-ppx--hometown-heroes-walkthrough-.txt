# GTA 6 Hometown Heroes Walkthrough

by [PowerPyx](https://www.powerpyx.com/author/powerpyx/) [Leave a Comment](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-hometown-heroes-walkthrough/#respond)

**Hometown Heroes (Join Billy on a Job)** is a Strangers and Freaks Mission (Side Quest) in GTA 6 (Grand Theft Auto VI). This walkthrough will guide you through all objectives of the **Hometown Heroes (Join Billy on a Job)** side mission.

* **Starting Location:** Kelly County > Thrillbilly Mud Club
* **Chapter:** 3
* **Quest Giver:** Billy
* **Requirement:** Finished Billy’s 4th side quest “[The Impaler (Steal the Impaler)](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-the-impaler-steal-the-impaler-walkthrough/)“, then wait a few in-game days until Billy sends a text message that marks this on the map.
* **Can Be Started By:** Lucia/Jason
* **Reward:** $10,000 cash & ![trophy](//www.powerpyx.com/wp-content/uploads/trophy.png "Bronze Trophy") The Lone Rider trophy/achievement (finished Billy’s questline)
* **Quest Description:** Billy’s Crew are waiting at the mud club. He’s planned a big job and is ready to move.
* **Questline (Billy):**[Thrillbillies](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-thrillbillies-check-out-the-mud-club-walkthrough/) > [Twist Wrist, Smash, Grab](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-twist-wrist-smash-grab-join-billy-on-a-job-walkthrough/) > [Mall Rats](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-mall-rats-walkthrough/) > [The Impaler](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-the-impaler-steal-the-impaler-walkthrough/) > **[Hometown Heroes](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-hometown-heroes-walkthrough/)**

**Objectives:**

1. Follow Billy
2. Drive onto the raceway track
3. Ram into other racers
4. Follow Billy & Loot the safe
5. Protect Billy
6. Get on the dirt bike & Follow Billy
7. Protect Billy
8. Go to the sugar factory
9. Find Lucia
10. Escape from the factory building

## Starting Location: Hometown Heroes (Join Billy on a Job)

Finish Billy’s first 4 side quests: [Thrillbillies](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-thrillbillies-check-out-the-mud-club-walkthrough/) > [Twist Wrist, Smash, Grab](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-twist-wrist-smash-grab-join-billy-on-a-job-walkthrough/) > [Mall Rats](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-mall-rats-walkthrough/) > [The Impaler](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-the-impaler-steal-the-impaler-walkthrough/). Then after a few in-game days Billy sends a phone message that marks his 5th and final mission at the Thrillbilly Mud Club in Kelly County.

This mission requires both Lucia and Jason. If you go there alone, your partner will automatically be called during the mission cutscene.
