# GTA 6 Collectible Guide – Progress Database

by [PowerPyx](https://www.powerpyx.com/author/powerpyx/) [Leave a Comment](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-collectible-guide-progress-database/#respond)

GTA 6 (Grand Theft Auto VI) contains around 3000 collectibles for all entries in the Progress Database Menu including Vehicles, Animals, Weapons, and Robberies. This collectible guide lists everything in the same order as the in-game Progress Database.

You can view the Progress Database under Map > Progress. Some sub-categories only appear after finding at least one item in that category. For the [100% Completion Checklist](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-100-completion-checklist-guide/) you don’t need everything, only certain collectible categories are partially required.
