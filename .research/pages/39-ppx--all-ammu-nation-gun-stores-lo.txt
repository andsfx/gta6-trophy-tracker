# GTA 6 All Ammu-Nation Gun Stores Locations

by [PowerPyx](https://www.powerpyx.com/author/powerpyx/) [Leave a Comment](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-all-ammu-nation-gun-stores-locations/#respond)

There are 24 Ammu-Nation (Gun Store) Locations in GTA 6 (Grand Theft Auto VI). Ammu-Nations are Gun Shops where you buy weapons, ammo, [body armor](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-how-to-equip-body-armor/), and [parachutes](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-how-to-get-parachute/). Each Ammu-Nation also sells a unique weapon variant, and sometimes even multiple. The unique weapon variants are tracked in the progress menu but they aren’t needed for the [100% completion checklist](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-100-completion-checklist-guide/).

When scrolling over their map icons it shows their type of weapon selection: Limited, Standard, Hunting, or Expanded. The “Expanded” stores carry the most weapons, including LMG, Sniper, Grenade Launcher, Rocket Launcher.

When [unfogging the map](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-how-to-unfog-the-map-fast-best-plane/), they get marked automatically with an orange gun icon . You can use the map filter to see how many you’ve found: Map > Ammu-Nations (Gun Icon). It will show a list of all their names with a counter in the bottom left that tracks the total amount discovered. You can even filter them by sub-type (Everyday, Premium, Hunting, Pawn). When you scroll over their map icons it also show what features they have – a shirt icon means they sell clothes, a weapon locker icon means you can access your full loadout there, a fish icon means they sell fishing rods. You can also check these icons in the map images below if you’re looking for specific features.
