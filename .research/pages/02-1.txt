## Overview

This GTA 6 trophy *Just Like Paradise,* is the first trophy you will obtain at the beginning of the game and it is completely unmissable with natural story progression.

Below is how to get through the *Just Like Paradise* segment, and where the trophy will pop for you.

Need help with other trophies? Check out the main page:

## Just Like Paradise Trophy

Progress through the story until the intro screen for *Just Like Paradise* appears.

Progress on the boat and pick up the drop offs that the plane made.

Keep progressing again until you have to watch Donnie from the sidelines with the binoculars.

As things go sideways, you will have to keep progressing to rescue Donnie.

As you make it onto land, you can make it through the area stealthily or by taking out the enemies with your weapon, keep making it through the area until you reach the shack where Donnie is being held.

Inside the shack will be an enemy that will jump you, take him out and proceed to rescue Donnie.

In the shack will also be some Zombix, pick this up to heal.

Once you make it out of the shack, take on the enemies in the area and make it to the caravan in the back with the dollar sign on it.

Inside, will be an enemy that will jump you, take him out, there will be some Zombix in here too, but make sure to grab the cash inside before making it back to the boat.

Make it out to the water and take the water plane and progress.

Fly away and complete the mission, keep progressing on for the trophy.

Once you make it back to land, take your car and drop Cal off at home, them make it back you Jason's place.

Once you have exhausted all of the cutscenes and Jason wakes up the following day, there will be a call from Andrew, Chucks Gun Shop.

Head outside and to your car and on the way, the Just Like Paradise trophy should pop.

This is the end of Gone Fishing as well.
