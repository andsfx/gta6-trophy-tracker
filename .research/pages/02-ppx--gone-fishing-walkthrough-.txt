# GTA 6 Gone Fishing Walkthrough

by [PowerPyx](https://www.powerpyx.com/author/powerpyx/) [Leave a Comment](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-gone-fishing-walkthrough/#respond)

**Gone Fishing** is the 2nd main mission in GTA 6 (Grand Theft Auto VI). This walkthrough will guide you through all objectives of the **Gone Fishing** main quest.

* **Starting Location:** Mariana County
* **Chapter:** 1
* **Quest Giver:** Cal
* **Requirement:** starts immediately after [Prologue](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-prologue-walkthrough/)
* **Can Be Started By:** Jason
* **Reward:** Jason’s House in Key Lento
* **Quest Description:** When a smuggling run goes bad, Jason has to rescue Donnie from the Kingfishers in Pelican Valley. He return to his house in the Keys where Lucia calls him and tells him she will be released early.

1. Follow the Plane & Collect the Packages
2. Search the camp for Donnie
3. Find a Way into the Shack & Untie Donnie
4. Head to the Plane
5. Get in the Sea Plane
6. Fly back to Brian’s Marina

## Follow the Plane & Collect the Packages

At the start of the mission you will see a cutscene of Jason Duval, one of the main characters you play, and his friend Cal on a boat. A Pilot called Donnie is dropping drug packages out of his plane and into the ocean. Drive after the plane and slow down near the packages so Cal can fish them out of the water.

After picking up the packages, follow the pink line on the minimap (bottom left of screen) to the swamps. Then equip the binoculars from the weapon wheel by holding / and selecting them in the bottom right slot. Look through the binoculars with / and zoom in with . Look at the deal taking place on the docks.

Donnie (our pilot from the plane) is meeting with the Kingfishers Gang. Things don’t go as planned and they capture Donnie – you must now infiltrate the camp to get him out.

**Tip – You can use Binoculars to Study Animals:** Remember you can use the binoculars to study any animals you come across. Select the binoculars from the weapon wheel, look at an animal, hold / to study. You need to study any 100 of the 255 available animals for the **[100% Checklist](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-100-completion-checklist-guide/)**, so be sure to work on this throughout the game whenever you encounter a new animal. You can’t see the 100% Checklist until after the story, but any progress made during the story will already count toward it.

## Search the camp for Donnie

Drive to the nearby docks and get off the boat. Jason now continues solo.

You can proceed in total stealth here by knocking out enemies from behind. Walk up to an enemy, grab with / and quickly tap / to put him to sleep.
