# GTA 6 100% Completion Checklist & Guide

by [PowerPyx](https://www.powerpyx.com/author/powerpyx/) [Leave a Comment](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-100-completion-checklist-guide/#respond)

Attaining 100% Completion in GTA 6 (Grand Theft Auto VI) unlocks **A Long Road** trophy and achievement. This 100% Checklist and Guide shows all requirements and how to complete them.

The 100% Checklist unlocks after completing the story. Progress made during the story already counts but you won’t be able to view the Checklist until after the story. You can find it under Progress > Stats > 100% Completion.

Nothing is missable for 100% Completion, everything can still be done after the story in free-roam.

The requirements are very lenient. It’s closer to 60% true game completion. The majority of side quests and strangers aren’t needed at all. Many collectible types aren’t required and others are only partially required. You only need to scan any 200 of 557 vehicles with the Waink app and study 100 of 255 animals which can be done by looking at them through the binoculars and doesn’t require hunting them. Though some hunting is still required for Bret’s questline consisting of 15 hunting quests. The only tasks that require everything are Robberies, Underwater Discoveries, Shooting Ranges, Drone Races, Bret’s Hunting Quests. The rest only requires partial completion.

* **Optional Missions** – complete Optional Missions as they appear. Optional Missions include the purple icons and the black icons with pink letters (whereas main missions are pink icons with black letters). Each of the vehicle deliveries of Moses and Randy counts too. Each Meditation Spot counts as +2 if you do it with both Jason and Lucia. There are a few missable optional missions during the story, but it doesn’t matter because there are much more than 100 optional missions total (easily 200-300+). You will automatically get 100+ optional tasks while working on all trophies/achievements. It’s still good doing everything at the earliest opportunity as the optional questlines often introduce you to new game features.
* **Scan Vehicles** – scan any new vehicles you come across using the Waink app by holding ![:up:](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-includes/images/smilies/up.png) and then ![:r1:](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-includes/images/smilies/r1.png) / ![:rb:](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-includes/images/smilies/rb.png) . For 100% Completion you need to scan any 200 of the 277 base model vehicles. If you start early you will get it done naturally throughout the story. Also scan boats and aircraft.
* **Study Animals** – study any new animals you come across using the binoculars. Hold ![:l1:](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-includes/images/smilies/l1.png) to select binoculars from weapon wheel, zoom in with ![:up:](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-includes/images/smilies/up.png) and hold ![:r1:](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-includes/images/smilies/r1.png) to study. For 100% Completion you need to study any 100 of 255 animals. Every breed of dog and cat counts separately too. Unlike Red Dead Redemption 2, you don’t need to hunt and skin the animals, just studying any 100 of them is enough.

## GTA VI 100% Completion Guide

### Story & Tasks

#### 1) Story Completion (finish the story)

Simply finish the main story. See [GTA 6 Full Walkthrough – All Missions](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-full-walkthrough-all-missions/).

#### 2) 100 Optional Tasks Completed

Optional Tasks include the icons with black background and pink letters (opposite of main missions which are pink background with black letters), and also the purple map icons. There are far more than 100, you will automatically complete well over 100 of these while working on all trophies & achievements. Each of the car deliveries for Moses and Randy counts as +1. Each [Synthetic Labs](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-all-kitty-litter-lab-locations/) for Tyrone counts. Each of the 18 [Meditations](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-all-meditation-spot-locations/) counts toward this too. Cal’s fishing challenges count. Side quests for [Strangers & Freaks](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-all-strangers-freaks-side-missions/) also count. There are probably 200-300 Optional Tasks in the game. A few of the optional tasks during the story are missable (for example getting Lucia clothes before she’s released from prison), but missing out on these doesn’t matter as you can do other tasks instead. So don’t worry about this at all, it’s practically a free requirement that you automatically complete while doing the other 100% requirements.

#### 3) 39 of 39 Gang Operations Cleared

![](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-content/uploads/gta-6-illegal-operation.png) Gang Operations (also called Illegal Operations) get marked by yellow icons on the map when you enter their location. There are 39 in total and you need to finish all of them, which means looting all key valuables at each location. Scroll over their map icon to see how many key valuables they contain. 19 of them get marked automatically from story progress. They respawn after a while and you can rob them again if you forgot a key valuable, so they aren’t missable but the respawn takes a long time.

» [GTA 6 All 39 Gang Operations (Illegal Operations) Locations](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-all-illegal-gang-operations-locations/)

#### 4) 22 of 22 Bank Jobs Fully Completed

![](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-content/uploads/gta-6-bank.png) Bank Jobs are unlocked after [Chapter 3 – Main Mission: Credit Check (Meet Raul’s Crew)](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-credit-check-meet-rauls-crew-walkthrough/). This mission unlocks the Dye Pack Jammer which you need to fence marked bills from banks. When unfogging the map the banks then get marked automatically with a green bank icon ![](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-content/uploads/gta-6-bank.png). You can use the map filter to see how many you’ve found: Map > Robberies (Money Bag icon) > Bank Jobs. Completed ones will have a bright green icon and incomplete ones will have a dark green icon. Small Banks always have a Cash Drawer and Safe which you must both loot to count the Bank Job as “completed”. You can scroll over each map icon and it will show which objectives you completed.

Hold ![:up:](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-includes/images/smilies/up.png) to pull out the WaINK app and hold ![:r1:](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-includes/images/smilies/r1.png) / ![:rb:](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-includes/images/smilies/rb.png) to scan vehicles. Just go to busy roads in different areas and scan all the cars driving by. You find different cars in each region, e.g. supercars around the hotels in Ocean Beach in Vice City, vans and trucks in Port Gellhorn, and basic vehicles in Key Lento. If you scan all vehicles you see from the start, you won’t have to worry about this at all.

The 280 Standout Vehicles (unique vehicles in fixed locations) don’t count toward this. You need any 200 of the 277 Base Models. You can keep track under Progress > Vehicles.

#### 2) 50 of 66 Standard Issue Weapons found (53 Guns, 9 Melee, 4 Explosives)

You can simply buy the weapons at ![](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-content/uploads/gta-6-icon-ammu-nation.png) [Ammu-Nations (Gun Stores)](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-all-ammu-nation-gun-stores-locations/). When you scroll over their map icons, some stores will show “Expanded”, which offer the full inventory including grenade launchers and rocket launchers. You can buy everything there. You can also pick up weapons dropped by enemies if you don’t want to spend money. Holding each weapon once is enough and you don’t necessarily have to buy them. The Melee Weapons and Explosives count toward the total too. You can keep track under Progress > Weapons.

#### 3) 100 of 255 Animals studied

In the weapon wheel select the Binoculars, zoom in with ![:up:](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-includes/images/smilies/up.png) and hold ![:r1:](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-includes/images/smilies/r1.png) / ![:rb:](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-includes/images/smilies/rb.png) to study animals. You only need any 100 of 255 animals (39%). This is a very forgiving requirement compared to Red Read Redemption 2. You don’t need to hunt the animals for this, studying them through the binoculars is enough (though you will need to hunt some for Bret’s 15 Target Decks in the Exploration category). It also counts each different breed of dog and cat. If you work on it from the start you will complete this naturally as you play.

Many cats and dogs are found around towns where people live. Lots of the wild animals are found in the swamps or the countryside in the north of the map. Fish count too and you can study them by swimming near them, so don’t have to use a fishing rod. Birds can be scanned too. You can keep track under Progress > Animals.
