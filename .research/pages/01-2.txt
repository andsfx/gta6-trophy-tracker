## How Many Achievements & Trophies In Grand Theft Auto 6?

**There are 55 trophies and achievements to earn in Grand Theft Auto 6 across PlayStation and Xbox.**

That’s slightly more than both Grand Theft Auto 5 and Red Dead Redemption 2, making GTA 6’s trophy list a potentially hefty undertaking for completionists. Of course, GTA 5 eventually received additional trophies through its GTA Online updates, so there’s every chance GTA 6 could follow a similar path after launch.

What’s more, both PlayStation and Xbox players will have their own prestigious reward to chase when working towards 100% completion. PlayStation has its long-standing Platinum Trophy, while Xbox’s new Mythic Achievement offers a comparable incentive for those who manage to complete everything.
