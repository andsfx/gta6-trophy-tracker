# GTA 6 Hit Kingpin Bowling Walkthrough

by [PowerPyx](https://www.powerpyx.com/author/powerpyx/) [Leave a Comment](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-hit-kingpin-bowling-walkthrough/#respond)

**Hit Kingpin Bowling** is an optional main mission in GTA 6 (Grand Theft Auto VI). This walkthrough will guide you through all objectives of the **Hit Kingpin Bowling** main quest.

* **Starting Location:** Kelly County > Port Gellhorn
* **Chapter:** 2
* **Quest Giver:** Lucia
* **Requirement:** Finished Chapter 2 “[Coffee Break](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-coffee-break-walkthrough/)“.
* **Can Be Started By:** Lucia/Jason
* **Reward:** Varying amount of money depending on how much you steal and sell to a fence. Can also steal unique Collectible Weapon “Gutterball Moreland 850” and Collectible Luxury Watch from the bowling alley.
* **Quest Description:** The bowling alley has been taken over by Kolt & Associates and could be a good next target to rob together.

**Objectives:**

1. Rob the Compound
2. Flee the compound

## Starting Location: Hit Kingpin Bowling

This is one of the 4 optional missions that will be marked on the map after finishing Chapter 2 “[Coffee Break](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-coffee-break-walkthrough/)“.

[![](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-content/uploads/gta-6-walkthrough-hit-kingpin-bowling-1-300x169.jpg)](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-content/uploads/gta-6-walkthrough-hit-kingpin-bowling-1.jpg) [![](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-content/uploads/gta-6-walkthrough-hit-kingpin-bowling-2-300x169.jpg)](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-content/uploads/gta-6-walkthrough-hit-kingpin-bowling-2.jpg)

## Rob the Compound

Call your partner for help to make this easier (hold ![:right:](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-includes/images/smilies/right.png) and ![:triangle:](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-includes/images/smilies/triangle.png) / ![:y:](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-includes/images/smilies/y.png) to meet up). Shoot your way into the compound – there will be many Kolts gang members waiting inside. It’s also good to bring 5 Zombix (bought at convenience store) and body armor (refilled at Ammu-Nation by talking to clerk and checking equipment menu).

Make sure you buy **sticky bombs** (explosives) at Ammu-Nation, this allows you to blow up the safe, which is needed for a trophy taking place here.

**Trophy/Achievement: ![trophy](//www.powerpyx.com/wp-content/uploads/trophy.png "Bronze Trophy") Let’s Go Bowling**(Follow-through at the Kolt & Associates’ bowling alley)– for this trophy you must loot all 3 key valuables in the bowling alley. Then the trophy unlocks after leaving the bowling alley and driving away, it’s a bit delayed.

**Key Valuable #1: Luxury Watch**
