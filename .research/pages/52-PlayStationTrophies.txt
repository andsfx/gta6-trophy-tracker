# Dime on the Dollar Trophy in Grand Theft Auto VI

- #### Dime on the Dollar

 

Earn $250,000 laundering Marked Bills through your contacts.

## How to unlock Dime on the Dollar

While $250,000 in laundered Marked Bills sounds like a lot, it really isn’t, because in order to achieve GTA VI’s 100% completion (see A Long Road ), you need to take down all 22 banks (14 regular banks and 8 “Big” banks) as well as all 8 vaults. You also need to launder $250,000 in Marked Bills for 100% too.

So, from that alone, fly past $250,000 (we got $130,000 on just one bank job earlier) – that’s provided you get out safely and can get to Yasmani in Little Cuba in Vice City, that is – you meet Yasmani in the 12th mission of Chapter 3, “Boobie Time,” by the way.

So, to do this, hit banks and payday loan companies, and then take the Marked Bills back to Yasmani. Don’t take it anywhere else, as you won’t get the true value for it. If you also hit the Payday Loans places as part of Yasmani’s “Payday Loans Jobs” optional missions, you’ll get even more value out of your robbery attempts. 

Once you hit that $250,000 mark, the ‘Dime on the Dollar’ trophy will unlock.
