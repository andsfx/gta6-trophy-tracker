# Catch Me If You Can Trophy in Grand Theft Auto VI

- #### Catch Me If You Can

 

Make an unscheduled departure at VCIA.

## How to unlock Catch Me If You Can

It wouldn’t be GTA if you didn’t wade into an airport and steal a commercial airliner, and that’s exactly what this trophy rewards you for.

First things first, VCIA can be found in west Vice City itself – it’s huge, you can’t miss it. And secondly, you can’t just grab any ol’ plane for this one. Oh no. You have to grab one of the huge double-decker airliners that's docked at one of the gates. You know you’re in the right plane when you enter via the tunnel used to board people onto planes, and then if you have to go upstairs to get into the pilot cabin. The plane is a Bottcher 020 – we first tried it with the Bottcher 015A, but that didn’t work.

Once you’ve done that, back it up, and fly it out of the airport and the ‘Catch Me If You Can’ trophy is all yours.

A word of advice, though: sure you can race through the gates and drive to one of the planes, but you’ll attract a 4 star Wanted Level instantly. Or, you could go to the car park near the terminals, climb to the 5th floor, and then drop onto the top of the passenger tunnel that people use to get from the car park to departures. Then you can climb the rooftops all the way to the plane itself. We did this and it was only when we pulled onto the runway to take off that the 4 star level popped up.

If you fight your way in, you’ll get peppered with bullets while you’re trying to reverse and take off. Death is a real possibility this way.
