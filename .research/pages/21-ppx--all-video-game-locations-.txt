# GTA 6 All Video Game Locations (Arcade Games)

by [PowerPyx](https://www.powerpyx.com/author/powerpyx/) [Leave a Comment](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-all-video-game-locations/#respond)

GTA 6 (Grand Theft Auto VI) contains 4 playable video games (arcade games). Playing each game once is required for the **[100% Completion Checklist](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-100-completion-checklist-guide/)**. Finishing all levels of one game unlocks the **Stick Fiddler** trophy & achievement.

Starting and immediately exiting a video game is enough for 100% completion. You only need to beat 1 of the 4 games for **Stick Fiddler** trophy / achievement. The first 3 can be played from the gaming console at the TV in your home. The fourth minigame is found at arcade machines in the world.

**All Collectibles Overview:**

* [GTA 6 Collectible Guide](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-collectible-guide-progress-database/)

## #1 – Tenebrae Lux

**Location:** Can be played at your home console. Interact with the controller at your TV in your home and select it from the JoySystem console. It’s still available in your home after the story, but temporarily becomes unavailable during Chapter 2. You can already play it in Chapter 1, then it’s available again from Chapter 3 onward.

You will automatically play this during [Chapter 1 – Main Mission: Feng Shui (Meet Cal)](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-feng-shui-meet-cal-walkthrough/).

## #2 – Street Trash III

**Location:** Can be played at your home console. Interact with the controller at your TV in your home and select it from the JoySystem console. It’s still available in your home after the story, but temporarily becomes unavailable during Chapter 2. You can already play it in Chapter 1, then it’s available again from Chapter 3 onward.

You will automatically play this during [Chapter 1 – Main Mission: Feng Shui (Meet Cal)](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-feng-shui-meet-cal-walkthrough/).

 

## #3 – Time Jackers Rewind

**Location:** Can be played at your home console. Interact with the controller at your TV in your home and select it from the JoySystem console. It’s still available in your home after the story, but temporarily becomes unavailable during Chapter 2. You can already play it in Chapter 1, then it’s available again from Chapter 3 onward.

 

## #4 – Bang For Your Buck

**Location:** This is the only video game that’s not playable on your home console. There are many arcade machines throughout the world that offer this game, for example outside the clothing store “Bargain World” in Port Gellhorn.
