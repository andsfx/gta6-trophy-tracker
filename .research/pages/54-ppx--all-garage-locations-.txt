# GTA 6 All Garage Locations

by [PowerPyx](https://www.powerpyx.com/author/powerpyx/) [Leave a Comment](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-all-garage-locations/#respond)

There are 12 garages in GTA 6 (Grand Theft Auto VI): 8 purchasable garages and 4 story-related garages. Buying all 8 purchasable garages unlocks the **Little Boxes** trophy & achievement. They cost a total of **$160,000**.

Shortly after finishing [Chapter 2 – Main Mission: Daily Driver (Steal the Crawler for Moses)](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-daily-driver-steal-the-crawler-for-moses-walkthrough/), you will receive a notification that a new app has been installed on your phone, called Vice City Real Estate. Through there you can buy all garages.

Each garage slot lets you register an additional vehicle. In total you get can a maximum of 18 garage slots (16 from garages and 2 are your personal vehicles parked outside where you left them). Some garages have multiple parking slots.

## 8 Purchasable Garages

These count toward the **Little Boxes**trophy / achievement. All of them are bought through the “Vice City Real Estate App” after [Chapter 2 – Main Mission: Daily Driver (Steal the Crawler for Moses)](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-daily-driver-steal-the-crawler-for-moses-walkthrough/).

### Garage #1: Mitchell Auto Center – $4000 (1 Parking Spot)

### Garage #2: 7th Avenue Garage – $12,000 (1 Parking Spot)

### Garage #3: Vela Rota Driver Garage – $24,000 (1 Parking Spot)

### Garage #4: Persistence Road Garage – $7,600 (1 Parking Spot)

### Garage #5: Wyatt Motors – $21,600 (1 Parking Spot)

### Garage #6: Bonita Court Garage – $34,000 (1 Parking Spot)

### Garage #7: Atlantic Court Garage – $40,000 (1 Parking Spot)

### Garage #8: Store n’ Bolt – $16,800 (1 Parking Spot)

## 4 Story-Related Garages

These are all automatic story rewards.

### Garage #9: Port Gellhorn Station Garage (1 Parking Spot)

Unlock Requirement: Finish [Chapter 2 – Main Mission: Daily Driver (Steal the Crawler for Moses)](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-daily-driver-steal-the-crawler-for-moses-walkthrough/).

### Garage #10: Thompson Court Garage (2 Parking Spots)

Unlock Requirement: Finish [Chapter 4 – Main Mission: Company Cars (Speak to the Concierge)](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-company-cars-speak-to-the-concierge-walkthrough/).

### Garage #11: NE 15th Street Garage (2 Parking Spots)

Unlock Requirement: Finish [Chapter 5 – Main Mission: Operation Savage Glory](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-operation-savage-glory-walkthrough/).

### Garage #12: Pegassi Residences Garage (3 Parking Spots)

Unlock Requirement: Finish [Chapter 5 – Main Mission: Operation Savage Glory](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-operation-savage-glory-walkthrough/).
