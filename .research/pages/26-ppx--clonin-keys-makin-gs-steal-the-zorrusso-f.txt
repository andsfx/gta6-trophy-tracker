# GTA 6 Clonin’ Keys, Makin’ Gs (Steal the Zorrusso for Randy) Walkthrough

by [Gage](https://www.powerpyx.com/author/gage/) [Leave a Comment](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-clonin-keys-makin-gs-steal-the-zorrusso-for-randy-walkthrough/#respond)

**Clonin’ Keys, Makin’ G (Steal the Zorrusso for Randy)** is the 60th main mission in GTA 6 (Grand Theft Auto VI). This walkthrough will guide you through all objectives of the **Clonin’ Keys, Makin’ Gs (Steal the Zorrusso for Randy)** main quest.

* **Starting Location:** Vice Beach > Ocean Beach
* **Chapter:** Chapter 3
* **Quest Giver:** Randy
* **Requirement:** Complete Chapter 3 “[Crosstown Traffic](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-crosstown-traffic-meet-raul-walkthrough/)“
* **Can Be Started By:** Lucia/Jason
* **Reward:** $21,250 + $10,000 bonus if car is delivered in good condition + Key Cloner (allows stealing high-end cars) + can sell Key Cloner cars to Randy + Randy’s Wish List optional questline
* **Quest Description:** Randy asks Jason and Lucia to steal a high-end car parked at an apartment building Ocean Beach. He gives them a key cloner to break into high-end cars and shows the list of specialty cars he wants most.

**Objectives:**

1. Install the key cloner
2. Search the area for the Zorruso
3. Steal the Zorrusso
4. Drive the Zorrusso to Randy
5. Follow Randy

## Starting Location: Clonin’ Keys, Makin’ Gs (Steal the Zorrusso for Randy)

After completing “Crosstown Traffic”, Randy will contact you and let you know that he wants you to steal another car from him, which is located in Hathaway Apartments in Ocean Beach.

When you arrive the area is a walled in parking garage, you can climb over the wall to enter the area.

## Install the key cloner

Once you enter the area you’ll call Randy, and he’ll tell you about the Key Cloner and the car having an advanced tracker. You’ll first need to install the Key Cloner on your phone. Press  to pull up your phone and select the bottom option “Install key\_cloner\_v1.6.9” which installs it for both Jason and Lucia.

## Search the area for the Zorruso

You’ll now need to search the area for the Zorruso. It is on the ground level on the east side of the area.

## Steal the Zorrusso

Interact with the door and then again to use the Key Cloner. Similar to using the Slim Jim, hold  in the correct direction when it turns pink to pull up the bar and clone the key.

## Drive the Zorrusso to Randy

You now need to take the car to Randy at his showroom.
