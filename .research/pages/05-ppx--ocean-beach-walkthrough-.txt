**Ocean Beach** is the 75th main mission in GTA 6 (Grand Theft Auto VI). This walkthrough will guide you through all objectives of the **Ocean Beach** main quest.

- **Starting Location:** Vice City &gt; Bayside
- **Chapter:** Chapter 4
- **Quest Giver:** Jason/Lucia
- **Requirement:** Complete Chapter 3 “The Cartel Heist“
- **Can Be Started By:** Lucia/Jason
- **Reward:** Room 701 at Y Hotel
- **Quest Description:** Jason and Lucia move into a suite at the Y hotel, organized by Valentina as part of her money laundering efforts. They meet the concierge, Santino, and Valentina tells them she will set them up with a meeting with hot shot Vice City lawyer Abraham Gonzalez in order to help them get new lives.

**Objectives:**

1. Drive to the Y Hotel
2. Enter the hotel
3. Meet with Valentina
4. Follow Valentina
5. Take the elevator to the 7th floor
6. Go to room 701
7. Explore your new suite with Lucia
8. Check out the wardrobe
9. Join Lucia on the balcony

## Starting Location: Ocean Beach

After completing “The Cartel Heist“, you have the option of immediately returning to the apartment to start this mission. If not, you start it the next time you return to the Bayside apartment.

## Drive to the Y Hotel

After the pair clean out the apartment, you need to head over to the Y Hotel on the Vice Beach side of the city.

## Enter the hotel

When you arrive the concierge will take your car, and you can head inside.

## Meet with Valentina

In the hotel head to the right side of the room where Valentina sitting.

## Follow Valentina

You can then follow her over to the elevator, where she will take you to the elevator.

## Take the elevator to the 7th floor

You can then take the elevator up to the 7th floor where the room is.

## Go to room 701

On the 7th floor head to the left to the far end of the hallway where the room is located.

## Explore your new suite with Lucia

Head inside and have a look around the room. Lucia will go through and open the rooms for you to look in.

## Check out the wardrobe

When she opens the door to the bedroom interact with the wardrobe, you have some new outfits starting in this chapter.

## Join Lucia on the balcony

You can then keep following Lucia around as she checks out the other rooms. When she goes out onto the balcony she’ll mention the champagne, and you can go inside and grab two glasses off the counter and bring one out to her. after which you will complete the mission.

This finishes Ocean Beach in GTA VI.
