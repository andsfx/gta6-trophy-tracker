# GTA 6 Credit Check (Meet Raul’s Crew) Walkthrough

by [Gage](https://www.powerpyx.com/author/gage/) [Leave a Comment](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-credit-check-meet-rauls-crew-walkthrough/#respond)

**Credit Check (Meet Raul’s Crew)** is the 59th main mission in GTA 6 (Grand Theft Auto VI). This walkthrough will guide you through all objectives of the **Credit Check (Meet Raul’s Crew)** main quest.

* **Starting Location:** Vice City > Crosstown
* **Chapter:** Chapter 3
* **Quest Giver:** Raul
* **Requirement:** Complete Chapter 3 “[Crosstown Traffic](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-crosstown-traffic-meet-raul-walkthrough/)“
* **Can Be Started By:** Lucia + Jason
* **Reward:**$6,000 + Dye Pack Jammer (allows stealing Marked Bills) + Bank Jobs / Big Bank Jobs / Payday Loans marked as Robbery Targets on map (green robbery icons)
* **Quest Description:** Raul invites Jason and Lucia to his base of operations in Stockyard and introduces them to his crew: Felipe, the driver, Karim, the tech expert, and Andrea, who manages comms and disguises. Jason and Lucia are tasked with robbing a series of small banks to get some practice before a bigger job.

1. Talk to Andrea
2. Choose a pair of masks
3. Talk to Karim
4. Pick up the dye pack jammer
5. Leave the planning room
6. Go to the Fleeca Bank
7. Park outside the bank
8. Enter the bank
9. Rob the bank
10. Leave the bank
11. Go to the next Fleeca Bank
12. Park outside the bank
13. Tell your partner to wait
14. Enter the bank
15. Rob the bank
16. Leave the area
17. Go to the next Fleeca Bank
18. Park outside the bank
19. Tell your partner to wait
20. Enter the bank
21. Rob the bank
22. Leave the bank
23. Lose the cops
24. Go to your apartment
25. Answer the door

## Starting Location: Credit Check (Meet Raul’s Crew)

After completing “Crosstown Traffic” you will be contacted by Raul to meet the crew. With playing with both Jason and Lucia together, head to the marked location near Vice Vinyl mod shop. Interact with the intercom on the wall.
