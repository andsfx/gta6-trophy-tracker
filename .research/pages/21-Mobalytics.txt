To obtain the Stick Fiddler Trophy in GTA6, you need to complete one of the four games that are in the game.

This guide will show you where the easiest game is to complete this trophy, along with where to find all the games to interact with and play.

Still need help with other trophies? Check out the main page:

GTA6 Full Trophy List & Walkthroughs

To quickly get this trophy, one of the faster games to complete is the Bang For Your Buck arcade game.

To find this, make your way to the Game Horde in Port Gellhorn.

Inside, you will find the Bang For Your Buck Arcade Game on the right.

Completing all of the sections on this game will get you the Stick Fiddler Trophy, it should only take around 10-15 minutes to finish.

If you are going for the 100% Completion trophy, you will need to as least interact with all four games, you don't have to complete them all.

As mentioned with Bang For Your Buck, that game can be found in multiple places across the map including at certain thrift stores, so if you found this game elsewhere, make sure you are interacting with it and completing it for the Stick Fiddler Trophy, unless you wanted to complete a different game instead.

The other three games are all on the JoySystem. In Chapter 1, you automatically try out two of those games with Cal at Jasons place.

You can launch the third game manually here, however if you don't use the JoySystem again in Chapter 1, you must make sure to launch the third game later on in the story to contribute to the 100% Completion trophy.

If you didn't interact with the third game in Chapter 1, when you get to Chapter 3, the apartment here has a JoySystem, make sure you launch the third game here instead.

Interacting with all four games is a must if you are going for the 100% Completion trophy.
