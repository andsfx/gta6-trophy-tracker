# GTA 6 - Capital Offense Trophy Guide / Achievement Guide (Banshee Car & Vehicle Showroom Location)

GTA 6 (Grand Theft Auto VI) Capital Offense Trophy Guide / Achievement Guide - Smash your way out of a vehicle showroom in classic style. [Bronze]

This requires stealing a specific car from a specific vehicle showroom and drive it through the showroom's window. The car you need is the blue "Banshee" from the showroom in the south of Vice City.

You can only steal it after obtaining the Key Cloner in Chapter 3 - Main Mission: Clonin’ Keys, Makin’ Gs (Steal the Zorrusso for Randy). Hack into the car, drive forward and straight through the window. The trophy unlock is slightly delayed and it pops after you drive further away on the street. The trophy only unlocks from this blue banshee specifically, not from any other car. It's an easter egg reference to the Banshee from GTA III.
