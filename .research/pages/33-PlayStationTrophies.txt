# Hot Together Trophy in Grand Theft Auto VI

- #### Hot Together

 

Lose a 4 star Wanted Level with your partner and escape the heat.

## How to unlock Hot Together

I actually combined this trophy with @Retail Therapy, because it honestly makes sense – and no, for some reason, we didn’t get it during the campaign, which is pretty mental… I assume it’s because I wasn’t together with my partner when I hit a 4 star Wanted Level… Anyway!

So, for this, you need to do 3 things: lose a 4 star Wanted Level; with your partner; and escape the heat. So, you first need to get to 4 stars, and then escape, with Jason and Lucia together. 

When we were doing ‘Retail Therapy’ (robbing 3 stores back-to-back), we were very conscious about keeping the police presence and not losing the Wanted Level, so we went a bit over-the-top and racked up 4 stars incapacitating cops. Firstly, that’s an easy way to rack up a 4 star Wanted Level.

Then, from the pedestrian street we used in ‘Retail Therapy’ in northern Ocean Beach, and with that trophy unlocked and 4 stars on us, we headed west through the alleys until we hit the large magnet-shaped building on the west coast of Vice Beach, which has a massive tunnel running through it. This allowed the helicopter to stop tracking us, after we lost the cops in the alleyways. Then, we jacked a car, headed south and took the bridge west to Waxman Island. Done.

Super simple, and 2 trophies for the price of one. You could say, those two trophies are ‘Hot Together.’
