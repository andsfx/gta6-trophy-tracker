# The Goodtime State Trophy in Grand Theft Auto VI

- #### The Goodtime State

 

Explore all of Leonida.

## How to unlock The Goodtime State

To unlock ‘The Goodtime State’ trophy, you basically have to unfog the entire map. Well, 99% of it, anyway – we unlocked this when we still had a few tiny unfogged sections.

In terms of what needs unfogging in terms of the map border, well, you need to do it so the top, right and bottom edges all have blue as far as the eye can see, and the left-hand edge has a thin strip of black. Fortunately, you only need to enter a sub-district, and it will unfog that area.

The best way to do this is to get a helicopter from any of the helicopter pads, and just fly to your heart's content. Your chopper will run out of fuel, so keep an eye on that – we just swapped choppers out at other helicopter pads when we were getting low.

Once you unfog 99% of the map, ‘The Goodtime State’ trophy will be all yours.
