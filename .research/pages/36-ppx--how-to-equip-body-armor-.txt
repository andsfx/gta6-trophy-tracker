TITLE: GTA 6 How to Equip Body Armor (Bulletproof Vest)
SITE: PowerPyx

Body Armor in GTA 6 (Grand Theft Auto VI) can be bought at Ammu-Nation Gun Stores. For example, the first gun store in Key Lento you visit during the story has body armor (bulletproof vest) on the right wall. You can also talk to the clerks at Ammu-Nations and you’ll find it under the “Equipment” section.

To equip the body armor, select it from the item wheel and switch it to “on” (hold L1/LB, click R1/RB and click X/A on the armor to turn it on/off). Then a blue bar appears above your health. It will automatically take the bullet damage to protect your health until it’s depleted. To refill the armor you must find or buy a new armor vest.

You can always fast travel to an Ammu-Nation by calling a taxi or using the RydeMe app once it’s unlocked.
