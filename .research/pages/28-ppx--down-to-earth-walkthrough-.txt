# GTA 6 Down to Earth Walkthrough

by [PowerPyx](https://www.powerpyx.com/author/powerpyx/) [Leave a Comment](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-down-to-earth-walkthrough/#respond)

**Down to Earth** is the 14th main mission in GTA 6 (Grand Theft Auto VI). This walkthrough will guide you through all objectives of the **Down to Earth** main quest.

* **Starting Location:** Mariana County > Key Lento
* **Chapter:** 1
* **Quest Giver:** Brian
* **Requirement:** Finished Chapter 1 Missions “[Clean Up After Donnie (Donnie’s Drop)](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-clean-up-after-donnie-donnies-drop-walkthrough/)” & “[Buck Me (Set Up Bank Account)](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-buck-me-set-up-bank-account-walkthrough/)“
* **Can Be Started By:** Jason
* **Reward:** None
* **Quest Description:** Brian wants you at the marina. Head over and find out what he needs.

1. Follow Brian
2. Clean the boat with the pressure washer
3. Go to the plane, fly to Bobcat Bay, land near the boat
4. Incapacitate or take out the enemies
5. Fly & land near the drops
6. Land near the dock
7. Hide the narcotics in the shed
8. Support Lucia

## Starting Location: Down to Earth

After the mission gets marked on the map, go talk to Brian in his office at the marina.

[![](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-content/uploads/gta-6-walkthrough-down-to-earth-1-300x169.jpg)](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-content/uploads/gta-6-walkthrough-down-to-earth-1.jpg) [![](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-content/uploads/gta-6-walkthrough-down-to-earth-2-300x169.jpg)](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-content/uploads/gta-6-walkthrough-down-to-earth-2.jpg)

## Follow Brian

Brian will walk with you outside to meet Cal at one of the boats.

## Clean the boat with the pressure washer

The boat is dirty and needs pressure washing. Interact with the pressure washer on the floor, use it with ![:l2:](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-includes/images/smilies/l2.png) + ![:r2:](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-includes/images/smilies/r2.png) / ![:lt:](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-includes/images/smilies/lt.png) + ![:rt:](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-includes/images/smilies/rt.png) until all the green dirt is gone. After a while Lucia will arrive and wants to see what you do at your “job”.

[![](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-content/uploads/gta-6-walkthrough-down-to-earth-4-300x169.jpg)](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-content/uploads/gta-6-walkthrough-down-to-earth-4.jpg) [![](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-content/uploads/gta-6-walkthrough-down-to-earth-5-300x169.jpg)](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-content/uploads/gta-6-walkthrough-down-to-earth-5.jpg)

## Go to the plane, fly to Bobcat Bay, land near the boat

Go to the sea plane with Lucia.
