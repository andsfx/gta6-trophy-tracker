# Let’s Go Bowling Trophy in Grand Theft Auto VI

- #### Let’s Go Bowling

 

Follow-through at the Kolt & Associates' bowling alley.

## How to unlock Let’s Go Bowling

In Port Gellhorn which is on the coast in central Kelly County, in the south of the town, near Moses’ garage, is the Kingpin Bowling alley, an Illegal Operation run by the Kolt & Associates gang where they run a pretty intricate pill operation.

To unlock the ‘Let’s Go Bowling’ trophy, you need to clear the location out of its Key Valuables. They include:

- Getting the valuables out the safe in a small room to the northeast.
- Getting the Luxury Watch, in the room to the south with the TVs in.
- Grabbing the Unique Weapon Variant – the Gutterball Moreland 850 Shotgun can be found on the pool table, in a large room to the northeast. 

Now, the trophy for us didn’t unlock until we left the vicinity (and got a Clean Getaway), so we’re not sure if that’s a requirement too. We assume not, as we’d hit this place multiple times, and wasn’t until we grabbed the watch and the Unique Weapon Variant that it unlocked.

Either way, grab all Key Valuables (in one run or multiple) and this trophy will be all yours.
