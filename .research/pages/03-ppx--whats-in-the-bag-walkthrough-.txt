# GTA 6 What’s in the Bag? Walkthrough

by [PowerPyx](https://www.powerpyx.com/author/powerpyx/) [Leave a Comment](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-whats-in-the-bag-walkthrough/#respond)

**What’s in the Bag?** is the 26th main mission in GTA 6 (Grand Theft Auto VI). This walkthrough will guide you through all objectives of the **What’s in the Bag?** main quest.

* **Starting Location:** Kelly County > Starlet Motel
* **Chapter:** 2
* **Quest Giver:** Lucia
* **Requirement:** Finished Chapter 1 “[Discharge Time](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-discharge-time-walkthrough/)“
* **Can Be Started By:** Lucia
* **Reward:** trophy/achievement Paradise Lost**![trophy](//www.powerpyx.com/wp-content/uploads/trophy.png "Bronze Trophy")**, and can now play as Lucia in the open world
* **Quest Description:** Jason wakes up in a Port Gellhorn motel room Lucia has checked them into. The dirty cop from the Visitor Center, Captain Méndez, is on the news blaming them for the attack. They look in the bag they took from the locker and see it contains an old st atue and a hard drive.

**Objectives:**

1. Read the note

## Starting Location: What’s in the Bag?

This mission is the start of Chapter 2 and it starts immediately after Chapter 1 “[Discharge Time](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-discharge-time-walkthrough/)“. After Lucia and Jason escaped the cops with the ambulance, they wake up in a motel. Lucia brought Jason there to recover while they hide from the cops.

[![](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-content/uploads/gta-6-walkthrough-whats-in-the-bag-4-300x169.jpg)](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-content/uploads/gta-6-walkthrough-whats-in-the-bag-4.jpg) [![](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-content/uploads/gta-6-walkthrough-whats-in-the-bag-5-300x169.jpg)](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-content/uploads/gta-6-walkthrough-whats-in-the-bag-5.jpg)

## Read the note

This is a very short mission. You will control Lucia and look at the things that were in the bag you stole from the Tourist Center. First, you look at a small statue of a woman. Flip the statue and read the note underneath it. The note says “From my collection. She reminded me of someone”.

Then you look at a hard drive, but the couple doesn’t have a computer to plug it in.

While Chapter 1 focused on playing Jason, Chapter 2 has you playing as Lucia while Jason recovers. The rest of the chapters revolves around Lucia taking care of Jason. You will also unlock the Paradise Lost**![trophy](//www.powerpyx.com/wp-content/uploads/trophy.png "Bronze Trophy") trophy / achievement** for finishing Chapter 1, although this mission was already part of Chapter 2.

This finishes What’s in the Bag? in GTA VI.
