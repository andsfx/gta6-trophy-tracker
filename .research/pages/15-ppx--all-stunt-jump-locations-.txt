# GTA 6 All Stunt Jump Locations

by [PowerPyx](https://www.powerpyx.com/author/powerpyx/) [Leave a Comment](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-all-stunt-jump-locations/#respond)

GTA 6 (Grand Theft Auto VI) contains 130 Stunt Jump Locations. These count toward the 250 Stunts needed for the **Cunning Stunts** trophy & achievement and the 300 Stunts needed for the **[100% Completion Checklist](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-100-completion-checklist-guide/)****.**

For 100% you don’t need all Stunts in a particular category, just any 300 of 397 across all categories (130 Stunt Jumps, 202 Under the Bridges, 40 Knife Flights, 25 Base Jumps). There are 97 stunts more than needed so you can pick what’s easiest for you.

You can track progress by region under Progress Menu > Activities > Stunts > press R3 (Right Stick) on “Stunt Jumps” to view progress by region. You can also track them by name on the Map to compare against the list here: Map > Exploration (Magnifying Glass icon) > Stunt Spots > Stunt Jumps > this will show a list of found Stunts (purple) and completed Stunts (white). They all have a unique name and stay marked on the map after completing them.

Each Stunt Jump has a small challenge, scroll over its map icon to see the requirement (use Exploration filter on Map). For the 100% checklist you don’t need to finish the challenges, just performing the stunt jump will count it as “completed”.
