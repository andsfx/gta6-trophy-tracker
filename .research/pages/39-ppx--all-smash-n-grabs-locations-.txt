GTA 6 (Grand Theft Auto VI) contains 35 Smash ‘n’ Grab Locations. Fully completing 34 of 35 Smash ‘n’ Grabs is required for the **100% Completion Checklist** and it’s one of the requirements for **A Long Road** trophy & achievement.

**Smash ‘n’ Grabs** consist of Pawn Shops and Jewelry Stores. To count them as “complete” you must rob each of them at least once. Be sure to enter the backroom and loot the safe too, if you just smash the display case it may not count as completed.

**Pawn Shops** are unlocked after Chapter 2 – Main Mission: Coffee Break. That’s halfway through Chapter 2.

**Jewelry Stores** are unlocked after Chapter 3 – Main Mission: Yasmani’s Jewelry Jobs. That’s halfway through Chapter 3.

When unfogging the map their locations will automatically get marked by green icons. You can use the map filter to see how many you’ve found: Map &gt; Robberies (Money Bag icon) &gt; Smash ‘n’ Grabs. Completed ones will have a bright green icon and incomplete ones will have a dark green icon. Smash the display cases to steal the jewelry and check if there’s a lootable safe in the backroom. You can sell the stolen goods to a fence.
