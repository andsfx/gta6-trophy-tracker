## How to unlock Personal Best

To unlock the ‘Personal Best’ trophy, you need to get both Jason and Lucia to level 4 strength at the same time. You can do this by visiting the gym and getting in a workout whenever your workout window is open.

To reduce the time between workouts, make sure to have a protein shake or something with protein in, after you’ve finished your workout. That will allow you to get multiple sessions going in one day – and seeing the gains. It’s a simple trophy in truth, because it’s beneficial to workout, as you increase your max health, so you’ll likely get this without even trying. We did.
