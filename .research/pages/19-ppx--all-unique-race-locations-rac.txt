# GTA 6 All Unique Race Locations (Racing Activities)

November 18, 2026 by [PowerPyx](https://www.powerpyx.com/author/powerpyx/) [Leave a Comment](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-all-unique-race-locations-racing-activities/#respond)

GTA 6 (Grand Theft Auto VI) contains 40 Unique Race Locations. Winning 20 Unique Races unlocks the [Earn Cash, Not Kudos trophy & achievement](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-grand-theft-auto-vi-trophy-guide-roadmap/). Winning any 30 of 40 Unique Races is required for the **[100% Completion Checklist](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-100-completion-checklist-guide/)**. There are 10 more races than you need for trophies / achievements so you can pick what’s easiest for you.

They get marked on the map with a blue icon when going within 100 meters/yards of them. You can use the map filter to see how many you’ve found: Map > Activities (Dumbbell icon) > Racing. Each Racing location features 2 or more unique races. Play one race to unlock the next. This costs an entry fee and a vehicle rental fee, but you’ll make a profit by winning the race.

They are listed here in the same order as the Activities menu (Progress > Activities > Racing) so you can easily compare what you’re missing.

Note: there is another category of races called “Race Challenges” – refer to the [13 Race Challenge Gold Medals](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-all-race-challenge-locations-gold-medals/).
