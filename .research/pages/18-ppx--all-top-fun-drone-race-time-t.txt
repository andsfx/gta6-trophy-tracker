# GTA 6 All Top Fun Drone Race Time Trial Locations

by [PowerPyx](https://www.powerpyx.com/author/powerpyx/) [Leave a Comment](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-all-top-fun-drone-race-time-trial-locations/#respond)

GTA 6 (Grand Theft Auto VI) contains 42 Top Fun Drone Races across 14 Locations. Winning 1 race at each location unlocks the [Top Fun trophy & achievement](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-grand-theft-auto-vi-trophy-guide-roadmap/). Finishing all 42 Drone Races is required for the **[100% Completion Checklist](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-100-completion-checklist-guide/)**.

They get marked on the map with a blue icon when going within 100 meters/yards of them. You can use the map filter to see how many you’ve found: Map > Activities (Dumbbell icon) > Top Fun Drones. You can also keep track under Progress > Activities > Top Fun Drones.

Each Drone Racing location features 3 time trials. You have to beat the par time on the first 2 races to unlock the third race. On the third race you don’t need to beat the par time, finishing the race once with any time is enough for the 100% checklist. So in total you need to beat the par time on 28 of 42 drone races (the first 2 at 14 locations). To beat the time you’ll want to hold the left stick forward the entire time to go at maximum speed and avoid crashing into anything.

They are listed here in the order they are closest together from south to north. You can call a taxi to fast travel to each location, except Dalton Island which is only reachable by boat or aircraft.
