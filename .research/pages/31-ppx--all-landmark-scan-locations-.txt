# GTA 6 All Landmark Scan Locations

by [HardyBoy](https://www.powerpyx.com/author/hardyboy/) [Leave a Comment](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-all-landmark-scan-locations/#respond)

Landmarks is 1 of the 5 sets of WAiNK Scan Locations in GTA6 (Grand Theft Auto VI). It contains 31 scan locations. Finding a total of 100 WAiNK Scans is required for the **[100% Completion Checklist](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-100-completion-checklist-guide/)**. You can track the progress under Progress Menu > Collections > WAiNK Locations > Landmarks. They are listed here in the same order as the in-game progress menu for easy comparison.
