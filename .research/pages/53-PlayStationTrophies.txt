# Treat Yo Self Trophy in Grand Theft Auto VI

- #### Treat Yo Self

 

Spend $100,000 across all shops.

## How to unlock Treat Yo Self

There are various things you can spend your money on in GTA VI, whether it’s weapons or clothing, or haircuts and vehicle mods. To unlock this you simply need to spend $100,000 across all shops, which you’re likely to do without realising it, especially if you’re modding your car for races and what not.

You can keep track of what Jason & Lucia have spent in the stats menu (Progress > Stats > Spending). Once you’ve spent $100,000, the trophy will unlock.
