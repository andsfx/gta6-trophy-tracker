# GTA 6 How to Unfog the Map Fast (Best Plane)

by [PowerPyx](https://www.powerpyx.com/author/powerpyx/) [Leave a Comment](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-how-to-unfog-the-map-fast-best-plane/#respond)

Fully unfogging the map in GTA 6 (Grand Theft Auto VI) unlocks **The Goodtime State**trophy & achievement (Explore all of Leonida). The best method is to use a double-decker crop duster plane from one of the airstrips.

While there are faster jets, they all have advanced trackers that automatically call fighter jets to shoot you down, and their fuel doesn’t last as long. The double-decker crop duster planes don’t cause fighter jets to come after you, and they have lots of fuel.

You can do this as early as Chapter 1. Below are 3 locations where you can find planes. Should they not spawn check the next airstrip. When your fuel gets low (yellow), land at the nearest airstrip to grab another plane, they respawn. Location 1 & 2 are near the start of the game in Chapter 1. You can [fast travel](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-how-to-fast-travel/) there by calling a taxi (choose Paradise Taxi from phone contacts). Location 3 is in the middle of the map, this is the best place to grab another plane when fuel gets low so you don’t need to fly to the south every time.

The map isn’t unfogged in a radius around you. Instead, the map is broken up into many small segments. Entering one segment briefly fully unfogs that area. It’s best to unfog one region at a time, then grab another plane with full fuel.

Remember to manually save your game from time to time while unfogging, as it doesn’t autosave often. If fuel runs out the plane will crash. You can press ![:down:](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-includes/images/smilies/down.png) to view the fuel gauge above the minimap (if you have it enabled in the UI settings).

**Plane Location 1:** South of map near Key Lento

**Plane Location 2:** Mariana County if no plane spawns at first spot

[![](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-content/uploads/gta-6-plane-location-4-300x169.jpg)](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-content/uploads/gta-6-plane-location-4.jpg) [![](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-content/uploads/gta-6-plane-location-5-300x169.jpg)](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-content/uploads/gta-6-plane-location-5.jpg)

**Plane Location 3:** Middle of map in Kelly County, great spot to grab another plane after you’ve unfogged the south.
