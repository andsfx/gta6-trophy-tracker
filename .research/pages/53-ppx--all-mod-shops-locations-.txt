# GTA 6 All Mod Shops Locations

by [PowerPyx](https://www.powerpyx.com/author/powerpyx/) [Leave a Comment](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-all-mod-shops-locations/#respond)

There are 14 Mod Shop Locations in GTA 6 (Grand Theft Auto VI). Mod Shops are stores where you can tune your car. Each Mod Shop specializes in a different type of vehicle so it’s good knowing where the specific shop for your personal vehicle is. You can buy performance upgrades as well as visual modifications there.

When [unfogging the map](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-how-to-unfog-the-map-fast-best-plane/), they get marked automatically with an orange car icon . You can use the map filter to see how many you’ve found: Map > Vehicles (Car Icon) > Mod Shops. It will show a list of all their names with a counter in the bottom left that tracks the total amount discovered.

## Mariana County

**#1 – Coastal Auto Care (Retro)**  
 Offers a range of custom parts and retro cosmetics.

**#2 – Capri Classic (Muscle Cars)**  
 Offers a range of custom parts and American muscle cosmetics.

## Vice-Dale County

**#3 – Two Wheel Fury Motorsport (Sports Bikes)**  
 Offers a range of custom bike parts and track-racing cosmetics.

**#4 – Euroform Automotive (Luxury Cars)**  
 Offers a range of custom parts and high-end cosmetics.

**#5 – Vice Auto Customs (Tuner)**  
 Offers a range of custom parts and street-racing cosmetics.

**#6 – Lowballers Customs (Lowriders)**  
 Offers custom rims and interiors, focusing on classic and antique sedans.

**#7 – Rideout Customs (Donks Custom Rims and Interiors)****– Ultimate Edition Exclusive**  
 Offers custom rims and interiors, part of the Ultimate Edition.

**#8 – Vice Vinyl (Vinyl Custom Wraps)**  
 Offers custom wraps and liveries.

**#9 – Stockyard Speedshop (Hot Rods)**  
 Offers a range of custom parts and old-school cosmetics.

## Kelly County

**#10 – Whisler Motorsport (Racing Cars)**  
 Offers a range of custom parts and track-racing cosmetics.

**#11 – Dirt Merchants (Dirt Bikes)**  
 Offers a range of custom bike parts and all-terrain cosmetics.

**#12 – Wasteland Off-Road (Off-Road Cars)**  
 Offers a range of custom parts and all-terrain cosmetics.

## Leonard County

**#13 – One-Eyed Willie’s Auto Shop (Off-Road)****– Ultimate Edition Exclusive**  
 Known for its custom, hand-painted liveries. Part of the Ultimate Edition.

## Lummox County

**#14 – Ledford Motorcycle Workshop (Chopper, Classic American Motorcycles)**  
 Specializes in classic American Motorcycles, offering repairs, upgrades and custom cosmetics.

For all other types of shops and collectibles refer to our **[GTA VI 100% Completion Wiki](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-grand-theft-auto-vi-wiki-strategy-guide/)**.
