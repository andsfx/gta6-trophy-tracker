## How many Trophies and Achievements are there?

There are **55 Trophies or Achievements that players can unlock in GTA 6** on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. On PlayStation, there are 46 Bronze Trophies you can get, as well as 1 Platinum, 2 Gold and 6 Silver Trophies. Meanwhile, on Xbox there is 1 Mythic Achievement, and you'll be able to add 1,000 G to your Gamerscore if you complete every Achievement in the game.
