# Retail Therapy Trophy in Grand Theft Auto VI

- #### Retail Therapy

 

Rob 3 shops back-to-back with your partner without losing your Wanted Level.

## How to unlock Retail Therapy

To get the ‘Retail Therapy’ trophy, you basically need to find a concentrated area of shops, and then hit them back-to-back-to-back while being Wanted. The good news is that Leonida is chock full of these very places!

- Firstly, there’s the Waning Sands mall (just south of central Waning Sands, to the east of Leonida), where you’ll find multiple jewelry shops, restaurants and clothes stores.
- Next up you have the docks in Downtown Vice City. There’s a strip mall there with loads of places to hit.
- Then there’s Peacock Bay in southern Vice City. In the northern part of that district are a load of clothes shops.
- There’s also the southern part of Vice Beach, where you’ll find a strip of 5 stores next to each other, just to the east of Lucciano’s poolside bar.
- Honestly, though, the best place we found (and where we got it) was in northern Ocean Beach (near the district border). There is a pedestrian strip of shops, restaurants and stores galore. There’s probably 20 at least.

Now, to get the ‘Retail Therapy’ trophy, you need to hit them as both Jason and Lucia, and not split until the trophy unlocks.

So, in Ocean Beach, we made our way to the aforementioned place and then shot in the air with a revolver. This alerted the police. The first police on scene came, at which point we started hitting stores and incapacitating more cops so that back-up came. We made sure we stayed in the blue and red circle with full white stars and always in sight for the most part – the full white stars means they know where you are and are actively pursuing you. 

Then, we just kept hitting stores, restaurants and even the jewelry store, until eventually the ‘Retail Therapy’ trophy unlocked. Nice and simple, and a foolproof method, because there are so many you can keep hitting. You’re basically brute forcing it, and it’s so much fun.
