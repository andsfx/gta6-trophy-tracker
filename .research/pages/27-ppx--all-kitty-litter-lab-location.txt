# GTA 6 All Kitty Litter Lab Locations

by [Gage](https://www.powerpyx.com/author/gage/) [Leave a Comment](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-all-kitty-litter-lab-locations/#respond)

GTA6 contains 14 Kitty Litter Lab locations. Finding and destroying these labs is required for Tyrone’s side mission chain, which awards the **Spin on This** trophy/achievement.

You are introduced to Tyrone automatically during the main story during the main mission Tyrone, then the mission Here, Kitty Kitty which furthers introduces the Kitty Litter storyline. After this you are prompted to work on destroying the labs where the Kitty Litter is made while finding out the main location it originates from.

Labs are marked on the map with a purple search area, where you then need to find the building where the pink gas is coming from. Inside the building will be a bunch of lab equipment which you can destroy with explosives or fire. You can obtain lab locations either by being given the locations by Tyrone or Dook, who will keep giving you more locations over time if you ask for them, or just by going to the locations yourself. Labs can be found without having the location given first, so you can just go to all locations and destroy labs on your own if you don’t want to wait for information from Tyrone and Dook. Some labs will have people in them making the drugs, who if you manage to stick up will sometimes give another location, but they normally attack you as soon as they see you.

After destroying a lab you will receive $2,000 or  $2,500 from Tyrone via BuckMe. After you destroy enough labs and obtain enough information you will be prompted to start the mission Spin on This where you help Tyrone destroy the main lab.

Labs are not missable, and can still be destroyed after the story in free-roam.

Labs are listed by region. There isn’t a way to check if a lab has already been destroyed other than by going to the location and looking, they will stay destroyed and the building will generally look burnt when you visit one you have already dealt with.

## Story Lab

This lab is the first one you are prompted to destroy before any others after completing [Chapter 3 – Main Mission: Here, Kitty Kitty](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-here-kitty-kitty-speak-with-tyrone-walkthrough/).

**Location #1 – Reward: $2,000**

The lab is in a trailer in the southern center block, spotted by the pink smoke coming out the top and the yellow tubes from the windows. There will also be two guards standing outside complaining about the smoke, before one enters the trailer.
