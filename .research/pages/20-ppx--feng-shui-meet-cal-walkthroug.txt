# GTA 6 Feng Shui (Meet Cal) Walkthrough

by [PowerPyx](https://www.powerpyx.com/author/powerpyx/) [Leave a Comment](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-feng-shui-meet-cal-walkthrough/#respond)

**Feng Shui (Meet Cal)** is the 10th main mission in GTA 6 (Grand Theft Auto VI). This walkthrough will guide you through all objectives of the **Feng Shui (Meet Cal)** main quest.

* **Starting Location:** Mariana County > Key Lento > Jason’s House
* **Chapter:** 1
* **Quest Giver:** Cal (via phone message)
* **Requirement:** Finished Chapter 1 “[Island Dreams (Work for Kenton)](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-island-dreams-work-for-kenton-walkthrough/)“.
* **Can Be Started By:** Jason
* **Reward:** None
* **Quest Description:** Jason and Cal hang out at home and rearrange the furniture to get ready for Lucia’s arrival. Jason has what might be his last night as a single man.

**Objectives:**

1. Sit on the couch
2. Move the TV / Coffee Table / Couch / Chair
3. Play Tenebrae Lux
4. Play Street Trash III

## Starting Location: Feng Shui (Meet Cal)

Shortly after finishing main mission “Island Dreams (Work for Kenton)”, you will get a text message from Kyle. The mission marker then appears at Jason’s house. The mission starts when entering your house.

[![](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-content/uploads/gta-6-walkthrough-feng-shui-1-300x169.jpg)](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-content/uploads/gta-6-walkthrough-feng-shui-1.jpg) [![](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-content/uploads/gta-6-walkthrough-feng-shui-2-300x169.jpg)](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-content/uploads/gta-6-walkthrough-feng-shui-2.jpg)

## Sit on the couch

Grab beer from the fridge and sit on the couch with Cal. He suggests moving your furniture around before Lucia comes home.

## Move the TV / Coffee Table / Couch / Chair

Help Cal move the furniture. After moving the Coffee Table you can choose to move it again or say it’s fine, but the outcome is the same. If you choose to move it again, Cal will be undecided and has you moving it back and forth a few times until it ends up in the exact same spot where you put it in the first place.

## Play Tenebrae Lux

Cal boots up the PlayStation-inspired gaming system. Now you get to try out two of the minigames. First up is Tenebrae Lux, a black-and-white first-person exploration game. Head toward the locked door on the right side and try moving up the hill. After some time the game automatically ends, you can’t finish the minigame. Jason gets bored and wants to play something else.
