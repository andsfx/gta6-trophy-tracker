# On the Spot Trophy in Grand Theft Auto VI

- #### On the Spot

 

Register 10 vehicles to the Scooter Bros app.

## How to unlock On the Spot

When you get into Chapter 2, you’ll eventually meet Moses, who will allow you to register cars – this is how you get your Personal Vehicle and change it from this point onwards. As the game goes on, you’ll be able to register tracked vehicles (first Standard, and then Advanced, if you get Randy the Mamba he requests) and then also register vehicles at a Pay ‘n’ Spray. So, to unlock the ‘On the Spot’ trophy, you need to register 10 vehicles – whether it’s at Moses’ or via a Pay ‘n’ Spray. 

Now, you can either get garages and store registered cars in there, or you can register and then sell if you’re struggling for space. Granted, you will lose money doing this, but it will free up space for a new ride to be registered, and then eventually, you’ll unlock the ‘On the Spot’ trophy when you get 10 of them. So, kind of worth it.
