# GTA 6 Welcome to Vice City Walkthrough

by [Gage](https://www.powerpyx.com/author/gage/) [Leave a Comment](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-welcome-to-vice-city-walkthrough/#respond)

**Welcome to Vice City** is the 44th main mission in GTA 6 (Grand Theft Auto VI). This walkthrough will guide you through all objectives of the **Welcome to Vice City** main quest.

* **Starting Location:** Automatically started after previous mission
* **Chapter:** Chapter 3
* **Quest Giver:** Automatically started after previous mission
* **Requirement:** Complete Chapter 2 “[Checking Out](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-checking-out-move-to-vice-city-walkthrough/)“
* **Can Be Started By:** Automatically started after previous mission
* **Reward:** Bayside Apartments, 201 (Chapter 3 Apartment), Pay ‘n’ Sprays buy and register cars with Standard Trackers (from Moses over text after mission is complete), ![:bronze:](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-includes/images/smilies/bronze.png) T\*\*s Ass Drug Money trophy/achievement
* **Quest Description:** Jason and Lucia meet Jason’s friend, Dre’Quan at Jack of Hearts – the strip club where he runs a record label in Crosstown, Vice City. Dre’Quan introduces them to his boss, Boobie Ike. Boobie says they’re welcome to stay in his apartment nearby. The settle into their new home.

**Objectives:**

1. Go to the Jack of Hearts
2. Enter the Jack of Hearts
3. Go to the back of the Jack of Hearts
4. Throw money to the dancers
5. Return to Dre’Quan when you’re ready
6. Go to the apartment
7. Park at the apartment
8. Go to the apartment

## Starting Location: Welcome to Vice City

This mission starts seamlessly after the previous mission Checking Out, as you are driving on the highway into Vice City.

## Go to the Jack of Hearts

After driving a bit in the city, Jason will call Dre’Quan and he asks you to meet him at the club Jack of Hearts.

[![](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-content/uploads/gta6-walkthrough-welcome-to-vice-city-1-300x169.jpg)](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-content/uploads/gta6-walkthrough-welcome-to-vice-city-1.jpg) [![](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-content/uploads/gta6-walkthrough-welcome-to-vice-city-2-300x169.jpg)](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-content/uploads/gta6-walkthrough-welcome-to-vice-city-2.jpg)

## Enter the Jack of Hearts

When you approach the club you will automatically stop the car out front. Approach the bouncer at the door immediately in front of you.

## Go to the back of the Jack of Hearts

You need to go around the other side of the building to the other entrance where Dre’Quan comes out to meet you. Follow him through the club until he stops at the stairs.
