# GTA 6 Child’s Play (Rescue Rafa from Kidnappers) Walkthrough

November 20, 2026 by [Gage](https://www.powerpyx.com/author/gage/) [Leave a Comment](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-childs-play-rescue-rafa-from-kidnappers-walkthrough/#respond)

**Child’s Play (Rescue Rafa from Kidnappers)** is the 94th main mission in GTA 6 (Grand Theft Auto VI). This walkthrough will guide you through all objectives of the **Child’s Play (Rescue Rafa from Kidnappers)** main quest.

![:bronze:](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-includes/images/smilies/bronze.png)

**Objectives:**

## Starting Location: Child’s Play (Rescue Rafa from Kidnappers)

**NOTE:** This mission is shown on the map as if there is a time limit where it can be completed, but you will just be forced to complete it when you have every other one of Jason and Lucia’s missions completed before the next mission “Power Play”. You can choose to do it before this point if you want, it doesn’t matter for the purposes of story progression.

After you have completed “Fresh Evidence” with Dre’Quan, Andréswill contact Jason about helping to rescue Rafa from the kidnappers. You can head to Megamundo HQ in Downtown as either Jason or Lucia to start the mission.

## Go to the quarry

After the initial scene, you will automatically be in a vehicle. Continue following the objective toward the quarry south of VCIA.

## Drive to the deal

After being stopped at the entrance, keep driving forward toward the deal location and Lucia will exit the car.
