TITLE: GTA 6 All Challenges Guide

GTA 6 (Grand Theft Auto VI) contains 80 Challenges. There are 8 categories with 10 challenges each. Completing any 50 Challenges unlocks the **One Last Thing trophy & achievement** and completing any 70 of 80 is required for the **100% Completion Checklist**.

All challenges are available from the start of the game. You can view which ones you’ve completed under Progress &gt; Challenges &gt; when scrolling over a challenge it will show a “Completed” checkmark in the bottom right corner.

While going for trophies and 100% completion requirements, you will finish most of these automatically. Then finish what’s left to reach 70 total. Since there are 10 more challenges than needed for 100% completion you can skip the hardest ones.

### Combat (10 Challenges)

01. Speed Kills – Kill 3 enemies in under 30 seconds.
02. Melee Combat – Kill or knock out 5 enemies in a row using melee weapons without dying.
03. Explosive Combat – Kill or injure 3 enemies with a single explosive.
04. Headshots – Kill 4 enemies with headshots in a single use of combat focus.
05. Drive-Bys – Drive-by kill 5 enemies in under 1 minute without exiting your vehicle.
06. Stealth Sniping – Kill 7 unalerted enemies using a scoped sniper rifle without dying or being arrested.
07. Incendiary – Kill or injure 5 enemies with fire damage in under 15 seconds.
08. Revolver Headshots – Kill 6 enemies with headshots using a revolver without reloading or switching weapons.
09. Long-Range Sniping – Kill 3 enemies in under 10 seconds with a scoped sniper rifle from 300 feet.
10. High Explosive – Kill or injure 8 enemies with explosives in under 20 seconds without taking damage.

### Criminal (10 Challenges)

01. Mugging – Rob 5 pedestrians.
02. Stick Ups – Rob 3 stores in a day without dying or being arrested.
03. Carjacking – Carjack 3 vehicles and take them to a Pay ‘n’ Spray in a day without dying or being arrested.
04. Mugging Spree – Steal $500 in cash from pedestrians in a day without dying or being arrested.
05. Cop Car Theft – Maintain a wanted level for 5 minutes in a stolen law enforcement motor vehicle.
06. Cop Weapon Theft – Steal a weapon from the trunk of 5 different law enforcement vehicles without dying or being arrested.
07. Stick Up Spree – Rob 2 stores in all 5 counties in under 20 minutes without dying or being arrested.
08. Cop Tow – Tow an occupied law enforcement vehicle for 1 mile without stopping.
09. Carjacking Spree – Register or respray a stolen vehicle from 10 different manufacturers at a Pay ‘n’ Spray in under 30 minutes without dying or being arrested.
10. Carjacking Pro – Earn $100,000 from selling stolen vehicles to any fence or Pay ‘n’ Spray in a day without dying or being arrested.

### Exploration (10 Challenges)

01. WAiNK Locations – Scan any 4 landmarks, curiosities, or sculptures with WAiNK.
02. Skyscrapers – Climb to the highest point of the 5 tallest skyscrapers in Vice-Dale County.
03. Collectible Cards – Find 3 collectible cards in all 5 counties.
04. Encyclopedia – Discover 100 animals, vehicles, and weapons.
05. Underwater Discoveries – Locate and explore any 25 unusual sights on the ocean floor.
06. Rail Tour – Take a 1 mile train ride in each of the 5 counties in a day without skipping.
07. Underwater Treasures – Find 85 underwater treasures.
08. Black Hole Wrecks – Find all 11 black hole wrecks.
09. Road Trip – Drive to every city and town in Leonida in a day without exiting your vehicle except to refuel.
10. Helipad Flyovers – Perform 100 foot or lower flyover of all 41 Vice City and Vice Beach helipads in under 10 minutes.

### Fitness (10 Challenges)

01. Gym Member – Become a member at any gym.
02. Foot Travel – Travel 5 miles on foot in a day.
03. Pro Cyclist – Cycle 15 miles in a day.
04. Pro Swimmer – Swim 3 miles in a day.
05. Balanced Workout – Complete a workout on each type of gym equipment in a day.
06. Strength Regime – Maintain Strength Level 4 with Jason and Lucia simultaneously for 5 consecutive days.
07. Cardio Regime – Maintain Cardio Level 4 with Jason and Lucia simultaneously for 5 consecutive days.
08. Waning Sands Run – Travel from the gate of the Tip pier to Waning Sands on foot in under 13 minutes without taking damage.
09. Tequesta Swim – Swim from Tequest Retreat to Starfish Island in under 15 minutes.
10. Southwind Cycle – Ride a bicycle from Williams Guest House in Graysn to Chicky’s Tiki Bar in Southwind without falling off in under 22 minutes.

### Shopping (10 Challenges)

01. Vehicle Modder – Make 5 modifications to your vehicle using a mod shop.
02. Grocery Shopper – Spend over $250 at convenience stores or liquor stores in a day.
03. Well Armed – Buy 3 sidearms and 3 longarms from Ammu-Nation.
04. Well Groomed – Spend over $1,000 at barbershops, hairdressers, or salons.
05. Stylishly Armed – Buy cosmetic upgrades for 5 sidearms and 5 longarms at Ammu-Nation.
06. Well Dressed – Spend over $5,000 on clothing.
07. Custom Ride – Spend over $20,000 on a single mod shop visit.
08. Heavily Armed – Spend over $50,000 at Ammu-Nations.
09. Serious Modder – Spend over $125,000 at mod shops.
10. Nest Egg – Bank $1,500,000 in your BuckMe checking account.

### Sports (10 Challenges)

01. Basketball Rookie – Score a basketball shot at 3 different hoops in a day.
02. Golf Rookie – Play 18 holes at either King Gator or Frontier Adventure Golf in under 30 minutes, finishing with a score of 3 under par or less.
03. Basketball Streak – Make 10 basketball shots in a row in under 2 minutes.
04. Pool Streak – Win a game of pool at 3 different locations in a day without losing or quitting.
05. Basketball Tour – Score a basketball shot in every county.
06. Golf Pro – Play 18 holes at both King Gator and Frontier Adventure Golf in a day, finishing with a par score or less.
07. Triple Bank – Bank the cue ball off 3 rails before potting a shot.
08. Basketball Pro – Score a basketball shot at all 100 hoops in Leonida.
09. 8-Ball Break – Win a game of pool by sinking the 8-ball on break.
10. Double Hole In One – Get a hole in one twice in a row in a single game, at either King Gator or Frontier Adventure Golf.

### Survival (10 Challenges)

01. Alligator Kills – Kill 3 wild alligators.
02. Big Fish – Catch a fish weighing 20 pounds or more.
03. Marksman – Earn a medal at both shooting ranges in a day.
04. Catch of the Day – Catch 3 fish using each type of lure in a day.
05. Zoologist – Study 20 different animal species.
06. Angler – Catch 4 different species of fish in under 6 minutes.
07. Pro Marksman – Earn a gold medal in the Mater challenge for 3 different shooting range events in a row without failing or quitting.
08. Hunting Spree – Kill a boar, rattlesnake, and a panther in under 15 minutes without taking damage from enemies or animals.
09. Pro Angler – Catch every species of fish.
10. Legendary Hunter – Kill all 8 unique animals.

### Vehicle (10 Challenges)

01. Vehicle Discovery – Examine or discover 6 different vehicles.
02. Wheelie – Perform a wheelie for 0.25 miles.
03. Winning Streak – Get 1st place in any 3 races using a muscle car, a sports car, and a supercar.
04. Throttled – Maintain a speed of over 100mph in a motor vehicle for 2 minutes.
05. Drawbridge Jump – Jump a vehicle more than 100 feet over a drawbridge 3 times without exiting the vehicle.
06. Top Speed – Hit a speed of 175mph in a car, truck, or on a motorcycle.
07. Train Landing – Touch down a flying airplane on a moving train without destroying the aircraft.
08. Oceanarium Jump – Jump out of an aircraft from a height of 0.5 miles or more, and parachute into the Vice City Oceanarium orca tank or dolphin tank without dying.
09. Rally Winner – Get 3 consecutive 1st place race wins in a single day at Thrillbilly Mud Club, Gellhorn International Raceway, and Boone Speedway without quitting or restarting.
10. Train Jump – Jump a motorcycle 75 feet or more and land on a moving train twice in 20 minutes.
