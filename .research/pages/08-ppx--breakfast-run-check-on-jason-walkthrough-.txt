# GTA 6 Breakfast Run (Check on Jason) Walkthrough

by [PowerPyx](https://www.powerpyx.com/author/powerpyx/) [Leave a Comment](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-breakfast-run-check-on-jason-walkthrough/#respond)

**Breakfast Run (Check on Jason)** is the 39th main mission in GTA 6 (Grand Theft Auto VI). This walkthrough will guide you through all objectives of the **Breakfast Run (Check on Jason)** main quest.

* **Starting Location:** Kelly County > Starlet Motel
* **Chapter:** 2
* **Quest Giver:** Jason/Lucia
* **Requirement:**Completed Chapter 2 “[Slam Dunk](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-slam-dunk-walkthrough/)” and wait for call from Jason
* **Can Be Started By:** Lucia
* **Reward:** Trust ![trophy](//www.powerpyx.com/wp-content/uploads/trophy.png "Bronze Trophy") trophy/achievement & both Jason and Lucia now have a personal vehicle.
* **Quest Description:** Head back to the motel and check how Jason’s doing.

**Objectives:**

1. Go to Hank’s Waffles
2. Rob the diner
3. Rob the liquor store
4. Rob the thrift store
5. Get in the car
6. Lose the cops

## Starting Location: Breakfast Run (Check on Jason)

After completing Chapter 2 “[Slam Dunk](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-slam-dunk-walkthrough/)“, wait until you get a call from Jason, then this gets marked on the map at Starlet Motel (motel icon turns pink). It takes half a day or so to get the call. If you have any other main missions pending, do those as well to trigger the call.

[![](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-content/uploads/gta-6-walkthrough-breakfast-run-1-300x169.jpg)](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-content/uploads/gta-6-walkthrough-breakfast-run-1.jpg) [![](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-content/uploads/gta-6-walkthrough-breakfast-run-2-300x169.jpg)](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-content/uploads/gta-6-walkthrough-breakfast-run-2.jpg)

## Go to Hank’s Waffles

At the start of the mission, you get a cutscene where time passes until Jason is back to full health. Him and Lucia then go on to rob several stores. First, drive to the waffle house.

## Rob the diner

Pull out your gun, aim at the staff and press ![:square:](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-includes/images/smilies/square.png) / ![:x1:](https://www.powerpyx.com/wp-includes/images/smilies/x1.png) to threaten them. They will open the cash register and put the cash on the counter, grab it. There will be a 1-minute timer in the bottom right corner (same for the next robberies), it’s more than enough time. Leave the store when done and you’ll automatically spawn at the next store.
