# GTA 6 All Fence Locations (How to Fence Stolen Goods)

by [PowerPyx](https://www.powerpyx.com/author/powerpyx/) [Leave a Comment](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-all-fence-locations-how-to-fence-stolen-goods/#respond)

In GTA 6 (Grand Theft Auto VI) you can sell stolen narcotics, jewelry, and marked bills to fences. Fences are special merchants that accept stolen goods.

Fences will be marked by a green icon on the map with their initials. They pay varying amounts for each type of stolen good. The available fences change throughout the story – new ones become available and old ones become unavailable.

## How to Get Stolen Goods (All Types of Robberies)

Stolen goods are found at the “Robbery” locations. On the map press on the money bag icon to filter the map for “Robberies”. Their icons are yellow, green, and some of the purple ones.

Throughout the story you will gradually unlock bigger types of robberies.

* **Illegal Operations (Narcotics)** – these are gang hideouts marked by yellow map icons. You unlock these throughout the story as you encounter each of the 9 gangs. There are 39 Gang Operations total. You often find narcotics there.
* **Jewelry Stores (Jewelry)** – these are unlocked in Chapter 3. Intimidate the staff, then enter the backroom and you can usually turn off the security system there. Then you can smash open the jewelry cases without triggering the alarm.
* **Payday Loans (Marked Bills)** – these have marked bills and are unlocked in Chapter 3. Aim a gun at the teller and he’ll hand out the cash.
* **Banks (Marked Bills)** – these have large amounts of marked bills and are unlocked in Chapter 3. Aim at the tellers and they will hand out the cash. Bank Vault robberies give the most money and are unlocked in Chapter 5.
* **ATMs & Security Trucks (Marked Bills)** – these are unlocked from Yasmani’s jobs (one of the fences) and contain Marked Bills in security cases. Shoot the cases to grab the cash. ATMs require a car with a winch to pull them out of the wall. Security Trucks require placing sticky bombs on their back door to blow them up (can be found in the back of Yasmani’s shop after doing his jobs in Chapter 3).

## How to Sell Stolen Goods

When you pick up a stolen item it will be stored in a bag that you carry with you (can also store the bag in the trunk of your car). Simply drop it off at the fence, at the green icon on the map. At the fence location you will find a container where you place the goods. In early Chapter 1 you will be paid in cash which arrives in the mailbox outside Jason’s home after a while. Later in Chapter 1 you unlock a digital bank account and will be paid via bank transfer when dropping off stolen goods.
